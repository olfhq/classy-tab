~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)          right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger                   p - thumb
2 - middle finger                  i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                    m - middle finger
4 - little finger                  a - ring finger (annular)

mezzo piano    mp   - medium soft
forte          f    - loud
fortissimo     ff   - very loud

Moderato   - at a moderate tempo
l.v.       - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
sempre     - always, still, continuing
m.d.       - right hand
rfz        - rinforzando - with a sudden increase of force,
             with a special emphasis
sub        - subito - suddenly
F.         - Fermata - pause, notes held
lunga      - long pause
lontano    - from a distance, in an eerie atmosphere
dolce      - gently, sweetly
muovendo   - with movement
breve      - musical note which lasts 8 beats (double whole note)
rubato     - expressive and rhythmic freedom (temporary disregard strict tempo)
sognante   - dreamy
rall       - rallentando - slowing down

h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
>    - accented, marked, stressed

<5>    - natural harmonic on the 5th fret
<7>    - natural harmonic on the 7th fret
<12>   - natural harmonic on the 12th fret
<19>   - natural harmonic on the 19th fret
<24>   - natural harmonic on the 24th fret
2[14]  - artificial harmonic, play 2nd fret, point to the 14th fret

|--3--|   - triplet     - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
|--9--|   - nonuplet    - 9 notes played in the time of 8 notes
|--11--|  - undecuplet  - 11 notes played in the time of 8 notes
