M            - ornament - lower mordent
s            - staccato - detached, cut short

F.           - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
breve        - hold the rest for the equivalent of 4 quarter notes

poco         - a little
sul pont.    - sul ponticello - near the bridge
arp.         - arpeggiate
libero       - freely
dolce        - sweetly, gently
sempre       - always, still, continuing
decresc.     - decrescendo - becoming quieter
cresc.       - crescendo - becoming louder
stringendo   - gradually faster, with more excitement
m.s.         - mano sinistra - left hand (hammer on the open string)
Piu lento    - slower
simile       - in a similar manner
dim.         - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
riten.       - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
cantabile    - in a singing style
rubato       - expressive and rhythmic freedom (temporary disregard strict tempo)
accel.       - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster, increasing speed
sub.         - subito - suddenly
pizz.        - muted form of plucking, with a shorter sustain
               (short, percussive effect rather than sustained)
nat.         - natural, discontinue a special effect

h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
/    - slide up
\    - slide down
>    - accented, marked, stressed
g    - grace note (ornament) - extra note(s) played immediately before the
       main note(s)
'    - breath mark, slight pause before playing the next note
v    - strumming from the highest note (5th string) to the lowest note (1st)
