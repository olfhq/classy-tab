Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)          right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger                   p - thumb
2 - middle finger                  i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                    m - middle finger
4 - little finger                  a - ring finger (annular)

CI      barre on 1st fret
CII     barre on 2nd fret
CIV     barre on 4th fret
CVII    barre on 7th fret

forte           f    - loud
piano           p    - soft
pianissimo      pp   - very soft
pianississimo   ppp  - very very soft
mezzo piano     mp   - medium soft
mezzo forte     mf   - medium loud
fortissimo      ff   - very loud

rall              - rallentando - slowing down
molto             - much, very
sul pont          - sul ponticello - near the bridge
poco              - a little
piu mosso         - a little faster
sul tasto         - over the fingerboard
tempo I           - return to the original tempo
ord               - ordinario - return to the usual way of playing
rit               - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
a tempo           - at normal speed
ten               - tenuto - to hold a tone or a chord firmly to its full value
sf                - sforzando - strong, sudden accent on a note or chord
sempre            - always, still, continuing
l.v.              - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
arp               - arpeggiate
dolce             - gently, sweetly
F.                - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
lento             - in a slow tempo
m.d.              - right hand (for playing the harmonics)
subito            - suddenly
main gouche seule - only with the left hand (for playing the hammer-ons)
seconda volta     - second time
accel             - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
Vivo              - in a lively, animated, brisk manner
sfz               - sforzato - with particular emphasis

bpm  - beats per minute (quarter notes)
h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
/    - slide up
g    - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
       before the main notes
>    - accented, marked, stressed

1[13]  - artificial harmonic, play 1st fret, point to the 13th fret
2[14]  - artificial harmonic, play 2nd fret, point to the 14th fret

|-3-| - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
|-2-| - duplet - 2 notes played in the time of 3 notes of the same value
