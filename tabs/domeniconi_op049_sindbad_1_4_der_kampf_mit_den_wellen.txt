Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The measure numbers don't follow the sheet, which only numbers the lines.

left hand fingering (LHF) above and below tab
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

piano        p  - soft
pianissimo   pp - very soft
forte        f  - loud
mezzo forte  mf - medium loud
mezzo piano  mp - medium soft
fortissimo   ff - very loud

Introduzione - introduction - passage which opens the movement
preludiando  - prelude, passage that precede the main musical piece
poco piu     - a little faster
rall.        - rallentando - slowing down
riprend.     - riprendendo - resuming the earlier tempo
accel.       - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
Mosso        - animated, rapid
Lento        - at a slow tempo
calmo        - tranquil, calm
maestoso     - majestic, stately
F.           - Fermata - pause, note(s) held for longer than their value
cresc.       - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
Molto        - much, very
sub.         - subito - suddenly, immediately
misterioso   - in a mysterious manner (e misterioso - and mysteriously)
Grandioso    - in a broad and noble style
cantato      - in a singing manner
sfz          - sforzando - strong, sudden accent on a note or chord
