left hand fingering below the tab -
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

===    - note held
\      - slide the finger down to the next note
cresc. - crescendo - growing louder
f      - forte - loud
g      - grace note - ornament - extra note played at the same time as,
         or just before the main note, then immediately released
mf     - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
p      - pull-off
