Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The order of play is measures 1-45, 16-24, 46-53.

measure 37 - in the sheet the top note of the 1st interval is C (not C#)

measure 53 - artificial harmonic, play 2nd fret, point near the edge of the
             soundhole towards the bridge (~27th fret)

left hand fingering (LHF)     right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger              p - thumb
2 - middle finger             i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger               m - middle finger
4 - little finger             a - ring finger (annular)

C(n)    - barre on the nth fret
c(n)    - part barre on the nth fret

D.S.    - Dal Segno - return to the sign ($ - start of measure 16)
al Coda - continue to the Coda symbol (+ - end of measure 24) then
          jump to the Coda (end section - measure 46)

arp.    - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
pulpe   - play with the fleshy part (of the thumb), not the nail
tasto   - play over the fingerboard (for a mellower sound)
F.      - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

<12>    - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
x[y]    - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret
