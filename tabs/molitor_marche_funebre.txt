Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
David Jacques doesn't repeat measures 0-16.

measure 15 - in the sheet the first chord is G-C-E-B (not G-C-E-Bb)
measure 23 - in the sheet the 3nd chord is E-G-B-E (not E-G#-B-E)
measure 24 - in the sheet the 1st chord is E-E-G-E (not E-E-G#-E)

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

f        - forte - loud
ff       - fortissimo - very loud
p        - piano - soft
pp       - pianissimo - very soft
cresc.   - growing louder
decresc. - decrescendo - growing softer

F. - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
h  - hammer-on
p  - pull-off
>  - accented, marked, stressed
