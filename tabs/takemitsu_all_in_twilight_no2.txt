Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF) above and below tab
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CI     barre on 1st fret
CIV    barre on 4th fret
CV     barre on 5th fret
CVI    barre on 6th fret
CVII   barre on 7th fret
CVIII  barre on 8th fret
CIX    barre on 9th fret

mezzo forte    mf  - medium loud
piano          p   - piano
pianissimo     pp  - very soft
forte          f   - loud

bpm      - beats per minute (dotted quarter notes)
arp.     - arpeggiate
poco     - a little
cresc.   - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
stretto  - quicker
in tempo - at normal speed
rit.     - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
Pont     - ponticello - near the bridge
Nat.     - natural, discontinue a special effect

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - slide up
\     - slide down
>     - accented, marked, stressed
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
        before the main notes

<12>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
2[14]  - artificial harmonic, play 2nd fret, point to the 14th fret
6[18]  - artificial harmonic, play 6th fret, point to the 18th fret
8[20]  - artificial harmonic, play 8th fret, point to the 20th fret
