Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
thanks to Christian Liang for the following corrections (28 August 2024) -
measure 22 - 2nd note changed to E (open 6th string) from A (open 5th string)
measure 30 - bottom note of last chord changed to G# (5th string) from D#

The tempo of the Rui Namora video & the MIDI is ~27 bpm (dotted quarter notes).
The tempo of the David Echenberg video is ~24 bpm (dotted quarter notes).
The left hand fingering follows the sheet - see the videos for alternatives.

measure 12: the sheet has C G Eb C for the last chord (not C G E C)
measure 14: the sheet has B D# for the 4th chord (not B D)

left hand fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinky)

===  - notes held, length of note, duration
c1   - part barre on the 1st fret with the 1st finger
C1   - barre on the 1st fret with the 1st finger
c2   - part barre on the 2nd fret with the 1st finger
C2   - barre on the 2nd fret with the 1st finger
