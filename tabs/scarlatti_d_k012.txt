Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

measure 7 - in the Symphonia-Verlag edition the 11th note is F on the 3rd fret
            (not F#, which is similar to measure 8 and fits the LHF) - but the
            Jovan Persec edition is closer to the keyboard score and repeats the
            9th note B (5th string 2nd fret) which sounds good - Gabriel Guillen
            in the reference video uses the Persec edition - (in the keyboard
            score the 11th note is an octave higher than the 9th note)

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

C(n)  barre on the nth fret

h   - hammer-on
p   - pull-off
g   - grace note (ornament) - an extra note played before the main note -
      the next note is usually played as a hammer-on, pull-off or slide
UM  - upper mordent (ornament) - play the main (melody) note, followed by
      the note above (in the scale), and then return to the original note

repeat section       [1. play the 1st time      [2. play on repeat
