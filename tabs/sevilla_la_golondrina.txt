La Golondrina (The Swallow), composed by Narciso Serradell Sevilla (1843-1910)
Also known as "Las golondrinas" - it is a very old, and popular, Mexican song.

The sheet music for the piano can be found here:
https://levysheetmusic.mse.jhu.edu/sites/default/files/collection-pdfs/levy-058-032.pdf
and here:
https://digitalcommons.library.umaine.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1545&context=mmb-ps

video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y-ojMrcNu78 - Eduardo Falu
video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S2bAh8Ytzig - Haroldo Forcillo

It has been sung by a lot of artists, including Miguel Aceves Mejia, Placido Domingo,
Pedro infante, Guadalupe Pineda, Nana Mouskouri, Mariachi Vargas, Nat King Cole,
Elvis Presley (changing the title to "She wears my ring"). It also was in the movie
"The Wild Bunch" - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0_A6tGZp6lY

Sorry, I have no dynamics, but if you know the song, I think it is easy to follow.
It is a sad and slow song.

Tuning: D A D G B E. Time Signature: 2/4. The original Time signature is  4/4
