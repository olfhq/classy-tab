Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The MIDI tempo is ~50 bpm (quarter notes) for measures 1-12, and 50 bpm
(dotted quarter notes) for measures 13-58.
The order of play is 1-44, 37-43, 45-53, 13-28, 54-58.

measure 30 - in the sheet the melody is D# G D# (not D# G# D#)

measure 45 - in the sheet the bottom note is A open 5th string (not E, open 6th)

left hand fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinky)

===     - note held, length of note, duration
/       - (below the tab) slide the finger up to the next note
s       - slide to the next note (rather than playing it)
<12>    - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
4<16>   - artificial octave harmonic - left hand 4th fret, right hand 16th fret
$       - segno - sign
+       - Coda symbol - jump to the Coda on repeat (measure 54)
al Coda - play until the Coda symbol (end of measure 28) then jump to Coda
bpm     - beats per minute
Coda    - the end section of a piece
D.S.    - Dal Segno - return to the sign (measure 13)
F.      - Fermata - pause - notes held for longer than their value
h       - hammer-on
Morendo - dying out, fading away
p       - pull-off
rit.    - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
Rubato  - the performer is encouraged to alter the length of individual notes
          though the overall tempo remains the same

c1   - part barre on the 1st fret
C1   - barre on the 1st fret
c2   - part barre on the 2nd fret
C2   - barre on the 2nd fret [etc]
