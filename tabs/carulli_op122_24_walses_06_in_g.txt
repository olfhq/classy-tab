Notes & Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The tempo of the MIDI is 144 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).
The order of play in the tab follows the sheet, but the Norbert Neunzling
reference video follows the pattern of most of the pieces in Op 122 and
repeats the first 8 or 16 measures (without their repetitions), which is
probably how it is meant to be played, here finishing at measure 16.

measures 16,20,24,28 - note the dotted quarter notes on the 3rd beat
                       followed by eighth notes

measures 25,29 - in the sheet the interval on the 3rd beat is C-E (not C-F#)

===  - note held, length of note, duration
f    - forte - loud, strong
p    - piano - quiet, soft
