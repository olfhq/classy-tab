Oremus (Latin for 'Let us pray') is a transcription of the second section of
Robert Schumann's Op 124 - Albumblatter (Album Leaves) No 5, Phantasietanz
(Fantasy dance). It is often preceded by Tarrega's Endecha. It was the last
piece Tarrega wrote before his death in 1909. Schumann's original piano work
gave the tempo as 'sehr schnell' ('very fast') while Tarrega's transcription
was published with the tempo 'lento' ('very slow'). (info from Wikipedia)

The order of play is 1-8, 1-8, 9.
The MIDI tempo is much faster than it is usually played.
