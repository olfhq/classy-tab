Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
"Campanella" is Italian for "little bell" - David Kellner looked after the
carillon of 39 bells at St Jakob's Church in Stockholm for 35 years. A
biography can be found at https://www.tabulatura.com/davidkellner.pdf

The original MS has "Presto assai" (very fast). The MIDI tempo is 80 bpm
(quarter notes) but it sounds fine at the slower tempo of 76 bpm as played
by Joseph Labute. (Note: Joseph Labute merges measures 11 and 12 together.)

The MIDI and the Joseph Labute video don't play the repeated s3ections.

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

#  - ornament - these can be played as (upper) mordents - play the main note,
     followed by the note above (in the scale), and then return to the original
     note - in measure 18 it is the top note of the chord that is ornamented -
     Joseph Labute plays an extended mordent in measure 29

c2 - part barre on the 2nd fret
c5 - part barre on the 5th fret
C5 - barre on the 5th fret
