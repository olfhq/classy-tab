Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The reference video brings out the melody far better than the MIDI does.
The order of play is 1-17, 1-8, 18.
The tempo of the MIDI file is 50 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).

left hand fingering (LHF) below tab      right hand fingering (RHF) above tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)            p - thumb
2 - middle finger                        i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger (annular)                m - middle finger
4 - little finger (pinky)                a - ring finger (annular)

===   - note held, length of note, duration
>     - accented, marked, stressed
+     - Coda symbol - end of measure 8: jump to the Coda (measure 18)
Coda  - end section of a piece
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra note(s) just before the main note,
        usually played as a hammer-on pull-off or slide - on a guitar
        it's often easier to play the grace note at the same time as any
        accompanying bass notes, on the beat rather than just before it
mf    - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
rall. - slowing down, getting slower (sometimes becoming gradually softer)
