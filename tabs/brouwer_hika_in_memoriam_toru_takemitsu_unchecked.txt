Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)          right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger                   p - thumb
2 - middle finger                  i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                    m - middle finger
4 - little finger                  a - ring finger (annular)                           
                                                   
CIII   barre on the 3rd fret
CVI    barre on the 6th fret
CVII   barre on the 7th fret

mezzo forte     mf   - medium loud
piano           p    - soft
pianississimo   ppp  - very very soft
forte           f    - loud
pianissimo      pp   - very soft
fortissimo      ff   - very loud

Tempo Libero  - freely
F.            - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
Andante       - moderately slow
bpm           - beats per minute (quarter notes)
legatissimo   - as smoothly and connectedly as possible
l.v.          - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
rit           - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
pizz          - muted form of plucking, with a shorter sustain
                (short, percussive effect rather than sustained)
a tempo       - at normal speed
poco          - a little
ord           - ordinario - return to the usual way of playing
Velocissimo   - as quickly as possible
molto         - much, very
agitato       - in an agitated, restless manner
Vivace        - at a brisk, lively tempo
Metallique    - near the bridge
gliss         - glissando
sub           - subito - suddenly
marcato       - with emphasis
legato        - notes tied together, they should be played in a smooth manner,
                without leaving any space between them
eco           - like an echo
sul tasto     - over the fingerboard
rall          - rallentando - slowing down
leggierissimo - very lightly, very gracefully
cresc         - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
sf            - sforzando - strong, sudden accent on a note or chord
accel         - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
marcatissimo  - with very strong accentuation
dolce         - gently, sweetly
come prima    - in the same manner as the first time

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - slide up
\     - slide down
>     - accented, marked, stressed
>>>   - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately 
        before the main notes

|-3-|   - triplet    - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
|-5-|   - quintuplet - 5 notes played in the time of 4 notes
|-6-|   - sextuplet  - 6 notes played in the time of 4 notes
|-9:8-| - nonuplet   - 9 notes played in the time of 8 notes

<x>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the x fret
