Legend
~~~~~~

left hand fingering below tab
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

===    held note
p      pull-off
h      hammer-on
/      slide up

(      arpeggiate - strum from the lowest string to the highest string
)

< - .  cresc. - crescendo - getting louder
. - >  decrescendo - getting softer
.      staccato
>      accent
ff     fortissimo (very loud)
f      forte (loud)
mf     mezzo forte (medium loud)
mp     mezzo piano (medium soft)
p      piano (soft)

meno mosso - slower
più - quicker
poco a poco - little by little
risoluto - bold, resolute, energetic

<7>    natural harmonic - play open string, touch point on 7th fret
<19>   natural harmonic - play open string, touch point on 19th fret

Performance Notes by Jean-Baptiste Rege
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

BAR 2 : --------0-2--- is also possible
        ------3------- far easier to play but you should
        -------------- try to sustain the melody
        --3h4---------

        also, at the end of the phrase, go to a half II barre

BAR 5 : play E with 4, then same for F# while you let go the
        II barre. Half barre for the D chord is optional, but
        maes it easier to transition to bar 6 imo

BAR 9 : if you played the (V) barre last measure, remember to
        lift it for -----7-
                    -5-----

BAR 10 : I'd try to only play the low D with p as the rest is
         melody and accompaniment. Playing the two low A with
         p as well is no big deal if they aren't accentuated

BAR 15 : you could sustain the A until the open E, it's just
         written as a quarter note as the D is part of the melody.
         If the note dies around the time you play the D, don't 
         bother lifting your finger, but if your guitar has a lot
         of sustain it's worth to keep the melody clear

BAR 19 : you could sustain the chord

BAR 20 : if the fingering is too hard, you can barre at VII
         and slide from C# to D with 4. Be mindful of the
         squeaking while to transitioning from bar 19 though

BAR 22 : to stop the D string, you can lightly touch it with your
         index as you play on the E string. Still, play all the
         bassline with p

BAR 23 : same. The fingering is tricky but really the only option

BAR 24 : to stop the bass notes again :)
         I would hover then 4 finger over each string. Takes a bit
         of practice but I think this is the most efficient way.
         Be aware not to touch any other string to keep the D chord
         ringing, you have to arch your finger a bit

BAR 41 : [ 7 ] indicates 7-tuplets, or 7 notes in the space of 1
    44   quarter note. Basically do the slurs as fast as you can 
         :)

BAR 52 : let ring the harmonics and the bass to extinction
