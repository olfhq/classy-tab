Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The tempo of the MIDI is 120 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes),
slightly slower than the saitenspuren reference video.
The order of play is 1-8, 1-8, 9-24, 2-24.

===  - note held, length of note, duration
arp. - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
f    - forte - loud, strong
fz   - forzando - strongly accented, stressed
s    - staccato - detached, cut off, note not held for its full value
