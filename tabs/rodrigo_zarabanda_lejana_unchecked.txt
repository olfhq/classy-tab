Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)     right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger              p - thumb
2 - middle finger             i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger               m - middle finger
4 - little finger             a - ring finger (annular)

CI       barre on the 1st fret
CII      barre on the 2nd fret
CIII     barre on the 3rd fret
CV       barre on the 5th fret
CVI      barre on the 6th fret
CVIII    barre on the 8th fret

piano           p    - soft
pianissimo      pp   - very soft
forte           f    - loud
mezzo-forte     mf   - medium loud
pianississimo   ppp  - very very soft

Andante quasi adagio - at a slow walking pace, but not as slow as
                       traditional adagio (evoking a sense of ease
                       and reflection)
rit     - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
Tempo   - a tempo - at normal speed
ten     - tenuto - hold or sustain a note for its full length
arp     - arpeggiate
rall    - rallentando - gradual decrease in tempo, slowing down
F.      - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - slide up
\     - slide down
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
        before the main notes
^     - strumming downwards

<12>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
5[17]  - artificial harmonic, play 5th fret, point to the 17th fret
