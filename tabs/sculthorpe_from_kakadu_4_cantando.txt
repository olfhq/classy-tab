Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CII   barre on the 2nd fret
CV    barre on the 5th fret

mezzo piano   mp  - medium soft
mezzo forte   mf  - medium loud
forte         f   - loud
piano         p   - soft

Cantando   - in a singing style, with a singing tone
espress    - espressivo - expressively
cresc      - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
poco       - a little
arp        - arpeggiate (poco arp - slightly arpeggiated)
meno mosso - less rapid, at a slower tempo
rall       - rallentando - slowing down
F.         - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

<x> - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
