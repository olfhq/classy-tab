Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
All three reference videos differ slightly from the sheet, with Kevin Loh
probably closest - he mistakenly plays C# (6th fret) instead of C (5th fret)
in measure 16. But at least he plays the correct harmonics in measure 27!

The starting tempo of the MIDI is 72 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).
The order of play is 1-13, 5-12, 14-22, 6-12, 23-27.

===    - note held, length of note
>      - accented, marked, stressed
/      - slide up to the note
< < <  - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >  - decrescendo - becoming softer
$      - (start of measure 6) segno - sign
+      - (end of measure 12) Coda symbol - jump to Coda on 2nd repeat
accel. - accelerando - accelerating, becoming faster, with increasing speed
Coda   - the ending - the last section of the piece
cresc. - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
D.S.   - (end of measure 22) Dal Segno - return to the sign (measure 6)
dim    - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
f      - forte - loud
mf     - mezzoforte - moderately loud, medium loud
p      - (below the tab) piano - soft
rall.  - rallentando - slowing down (sometimes becoming gradually softer)
rit.   - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
t      - tenuto - slightly stressed, held for a little longer than its value
tempo  - speed (a tempo - return to the earlier speed)

<5>   - natural (open) (double octave) harmonic on the 5th fret
<12>  - natural (open) (octave) harmonic on the 12th fret
2<14> - artificial harmonic - left hand 2nd fret, right hand points to 14th fret
3<15> - artificial harmonic - left hand 3rd fret, right hand points to 15th fret

c1 - part barre on the 1st fret
C1 - barre on the 1st fret
c2 - part barre on the 2nd fret
C2 - barre on the 2nd fret [etc]

repeat the section      1____  play the 1st time       2_______  play on repeat

left hand fingering (LHF)     right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger              p - thumb
2 - middle finger             i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger               m - middle finger
4 - little finger             a - ring finger (annular)

CI      barre on the 1st fret
CII     barre on the 2nd fret
CIII    barre on the 3rd fret

CV      barre on the 5th fret
CVI     barre on the 6th fret
CVIII   barre on the 8th fret
[       barre with the index finger

pianissimo      pp   - very soft
piano           p    - soft
mezzo-piano     mp   - medium soft
mezzo-forte     mf   - medium loud
forte           f    - loud
pianississimo   ppp  - very very soft

lontano     - from a distance, as from far away
tastiera    - over the fingerboard
r.h.        - play with the right hand
arp         - arpeggiate
l.v.        - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
vib         - vibrato
gliss       - glissando
pont        - ponticello - near the bridge
sub         - subito - suddenly
F.          - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
sfz         - sforzado - strong, sudden accent on a note or chord
tamb        - tambour / tambora - the strings are played all at once by hitting them
              near the bridge with a flat part of the hand (usually with the side of
              the outstretched thumb)
sereno      - in a tranquil, serene manner
Ossia       - alternative passage may be played
              instead of the original passage

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - slide up
\     - slide down
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
        before the main notes
^     - strumming downwards
>     - accented, marked, stressed
(x)   - play with the left hand only

<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret
