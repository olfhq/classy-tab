Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)     right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger              p - thumb
2 - middle finger             i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger               m - middle finger
4 - little finger             a - ring finger (annular)

CI       barre on the 1st fret
CII      barre on the 2nd fret
CIII     barre on the 3rd fret
CIV      barre on the 4th fret
CV       barre on the 5th fret
CVI      barre on the 6th fret
CVII     barre on the 7th fret
CVIII    barre on the 8th fret
CX       barre on the 10th fret
[        barre with the index finger

arp.   - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest
F.     - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
ten.   - tenuto  - notes played firmly, held for at least their full value
rall.  - rallentando -slowing down

h      - hammer-on
p      - pull-off
/      - slide up
\      - slide down
g      - grace note (ornament) - extra note(s) played immediately
         before the main notes
'      - (measure 81) slight pause, breathmark
|-3-|  - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
