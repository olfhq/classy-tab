Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The order of play is measures 0-6, 1-5, 7-13, 8-12, 14-25.
The tempo of the MIDI is 88 bpm (beats per minute - dotted quarter notes).

measure 13,14 - in the sheet there is a pull-off from the 4th note (A) to the G
                on the 8th fret, but the A is held in all 3 reference videos

Left Hand Fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger (pinkie)

===  - length of note, note held, duration
/    - (below the  tab) move the same finger up to the next note
h    - hammer-on
mf   - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
mp   - mezzo-piano - moderately soft
p    - (between the notes) pull-off
p    - (below the tab) piano - soft
pp   - pianissimo - very soft
ppp  - pianississimo - extremely soft

c1 - part barre on the first fret
C1 - barre on the 1st fret
c2 - part barre on the 2nd fret
C2 - barre on the 2nd fret [etc]
