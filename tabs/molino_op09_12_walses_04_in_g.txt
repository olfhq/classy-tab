Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Two of Molino's compositions are called "Op 9". Gambino originally published
'12 Themes varies pour guitare seule' as Op 9 (also known as '12 Variations on
"Ah! Vous dirai-je maman" - 'Twinkle Twinkle Little Star') and '24 Valtz pour
la guitare" as Op 10. However Op 10 was published in two volumes of 12 waltzes,
and Breitkopf & Hartel republished the first 12 waltzes as Op 9, and the last
12 waltzes as Op 10. (Thanks to Pierre Schneider for this information.)

The tempo of the MIDI is 180 bpm (beats per minute - eighth notes).
The sheet does not indicate the final repeat of the first sixteen measures.
Though the key signature does not change, measures 17-32 are in the key of C.
The order of play is 0-8, 0-8, 9-16, 9-16, 17-24, 17-24, 25-32, 25-32, 0-16.

===          - note held, length of note, duration
Fine         - end of the piece
h            - hammer-on
p            - pull-off
D.C. al Fine - return to the start and continue until Fine
