Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
"While I have indicated many of the fingerings I use, I invite guitarists
to explore other options. Similarly, I feel that interpretation, dynamics,
tempi, and phrasing should be guided not only by the composer but also by
the performer. I am often delighted when I hear a performance of my music
that includes new expressive ideas. The indicated tempi are suggestions,
not specific requirements." - Matthew Dunne

This is not an easy piece to play. There are difficult stretches, some
discords and subtle changes of rhythm. The time signature in the sheet is 4/4,
but the rhythm within the measures is mostly 3+3+2 eighth notes (rather than
the 2+2+2+2 eight notes). Measures 38 & 39 seem especially discordant - check
the Michael Cohen video on how to minimise the damage / enhance the beauty.

measure 16 - the last 2 notes are sixteenth notes
