Notes and Legend

'Floreando' (Spanish for 'flowers') is used in the sense of spreading flowers.

left hand fingering (LHF)     right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger              p - thumb
2 - middle finger             i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger               m - middle finger
4 - little finger             a - ring finger (annular)

mezzo-forte    mf  - medium loud
forte          f   - loud
piano          p   - soft
fortissimo     ff  - very loud
pianissimo     pp  - very soft

bpm         - beats per minute (quarter notes)
dim.        - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
cresc.      - crescendo - becoming louder
decresc.    - decrescendo - becoming softer
poco rall.  - poco rallentando - slowing down a little
Tempo I     - Tempo primo - return to the original tempo
senza rall. - senza rallentando - without slowing down
arp.        - arpeggiate
F.          - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

<7>  - natural (open) harmonic on the 7th fret
