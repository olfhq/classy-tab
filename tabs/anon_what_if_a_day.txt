Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
This English Renaissance song was originally composed for lute, and is 
sometimes attributed to Thomas Campion (1567-1620). It was published in
Richard Alison's 'An Howres Recreation in Musicke' in 1606.
