Passagalia in G Minor - Heinrich I F von Biber (1644-1704)
**********************************************************

From the Rosary Sonatas
Based on score published by Johan Tufvesson for solo Violin -
https://www.lysator.liu.se/~tuben/scores/
I attempt to play this with barres at the 3rd and 5th frets as my fingers
are short and getting old. There is a good classical guitar rendition by
David Massey on YouTube ("in drop D and G tuning") -
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7E1vxQAydo4
- R M Buchanan

last update: January 2025 (better left hand fingering)

standard tuning: E A D G B E       tr = trill
