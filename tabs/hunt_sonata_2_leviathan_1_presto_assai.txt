Performance notes
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Measure 35: The sheet music indicates you should move finger 1 to play both of
            these notes with no glissando indicated. I prefer to finger G with
            3 and play Bb and A with 2 and 1.

Measure 44: The sheet music indicates an F on the high E string, but this seems
            like an error given the necessary fingering.

===    held note
p      pull-off
h      hammer-on
g      grace note(s)
//     the end of a metrical phrase with any pause at the discretion
       of the performer

< - -  crescendo
- - >  decrescendo

(      arpeggiate

.      staccato
