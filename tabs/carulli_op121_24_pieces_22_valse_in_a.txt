Notes & Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
This melody was used for a traditional Northern England early C20th song
"Dun't Pu' Thi Muck In Aah Dus'bin" (also known as the 'Barnsley Anthem').
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HXr76x_4KDM - Tony Casey (with lyrics)

Tempo: the Norman Nenuzling video is 152 bpm (beats per minute - quarter
notes); the Uros Bari audio is 176 bpm; the MIDI file is 160 bpm.
