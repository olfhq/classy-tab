Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

pianissimo      pp   - very soft
pianississimo   ppp  - extremely soft
piano           p    - soft

CII      barre on the 2nd fret
CIII     barre on the 3rd fret
CIV      barre on the 4th fret
CV       barre on the 5th fret
CVII     barre on the 7th fret
CVIII    barre on the 8th fret

Andante          - moderately slow
bpm              - beats per minute (quarter notes)
poco             - a little
cresc.           - crescendo - becoming louder
rit.             - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
a tempo          - at normal speed
dim.             - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
F.               - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
Moderato         - at a moderate pace
lo stesso tempo  - the same tempo
poco piu         - a little faster
grave            - slow and solemn
meno p           - less softly
molto            - much, very
Lento            - at a slow tempo
Como 1a          - in the same manner as before (repeat measures 1-10)

h      - hammer-on
p      - pull-off
/      - slide up
\      - slide down
< < <  - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >  - decrescendo - becoming softer
g      - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately before
         the main notes, usually played as hammer-ons, pull-offs or slides
