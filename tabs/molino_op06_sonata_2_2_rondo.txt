Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
On the piano, grace notes are often played just before the beat; on the
guitar it is usually easier to play them on the beat.

The tempo of the MIDI is 68 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).

===      - note held, length of note, duration
> > >    - decrescendo - becoming softer
Adagio   - slow
cresc.   - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
dolce    - sweetly
espress. - expression (con espress. - with expression)
f        - forte - loud, strong
ff       - fortissimo - very loud
F.       - Fermata - pause, note(s) held for longer than their value
g        - grace note, ornament - extra note played just before the main note -
           the next note is usually played as a hammer-on, pull-off or slide
p        - (below the tab) piano - soft, quietly
p        - (between the notes) pull-off
ritard.  - slowing down
sf       - sforzando - suddenly louder
tempo    - speed (a tempo - return to the earlier tempo)
