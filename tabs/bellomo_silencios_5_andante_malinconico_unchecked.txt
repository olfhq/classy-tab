Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering         right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger            p - thumb
2 - middle finger           i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger             m - middle finger
4 - little finger           a - ring finger (annular)

CII    barre on 2nd fret
CV     barre on 5th fret
CVII   barre on 7th fret

mezzo piano    mp  - medium soft
mezzo forte    mf  - medium loud

Andante      - moderately slow
malinconico  - in a melancholy style
espress      - expressively
legato       - notes tied together, they should be played in a smooth manner,
               without leaving any space between them
leggero      - with a light, delicate, graceful touch
F.           - Fermata - pause, notes held
rubato       - expressive and rhythmic freedom (temporary disregard strict tempo)
cresc        - crescendo - becoming louder
marcato      - with emphasis
profondo     - profound, deep

<12>   - natural harmonic on the 12th fret
<19>   - natural harmonic on the 19th fret
3[15]  - artificial harmonic, play 3rd fret, point to the 15th fret

|-3-| - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
