left hand fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinkie)

===    - length of note, note held, duration
/      - slide the note up
>      - accented, marked, stressed
< < <  - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >  - decrescendo - becoming softer
bpm    - beats per minute
F.     - Fermata - pause (wait before starting the next Prelude in the set)
poco   - little (poco rit. - a little slower)
rit.   - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
tempo  - speed (a tempo - return to the earlier tempo)

<12>   - natural (open) octave harmonic - point to the 12th fret
1<13>  - artficial harmonic - play 1st fret, point to the 13th fret
2<14>  - artficial harmonic - play 2nd fret, point to the 14th fret
3<15>  - artficial harmonic - play 3rd fret, point to the 15th fret

c1     - part barre on  the 1st fret
C1     - barre on  the 1st fret
c2     - part barre on  the 2nd fret
C2     - barre on  the 2nd fret [etc]
