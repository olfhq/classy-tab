Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
order of play - measures 1-37, 1-26, 38-59

left hand fingering
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CII    barre on the 2nd fret
CIII   barre on the 3rd fret
CV     barre on the 5th fret
CVII   barre on the 7th fret
CX     barre on the 10th fret

mezzo forte    mf  - medium loud
mezzo piano    mp  - medium soft
forte          f   - loud
piano          p   - soft

bpm          - beats per minute (quarter notes)
rit.         - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
F.           - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
M            - mordent (ornament) - play main note, note above, then main note
poco         - a little (poco animato - a little livelier)
a tempo      - return to the normal speed (after slowing down)
vib.         - vibrato - enriching the sound of a note by vibrating the finger
               laterally along the direction of the string so that the sound
               alternates between the main note and slightly below the main note
animato      - in an animated, lively manner
Coda         - passage that brings the movement to an end
gliss.       - glissando
D.C. al Coda - Da Capo al Coda - from the beginning to the Coda (m.26)

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - slide up
\     - slide down
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra note played just before the main note

|-3-| - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes

<x>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret
r.h.  - harmonic played with the right hand
