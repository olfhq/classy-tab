left hand fingering (LHF) above and below tab      right hand fingering (RHF)

CI    - barre on 1st fret

CVII  - barre on 7th fret

mezzo-piano    mp  - medium soft
pianissimo     pp  - very soft
piano          p   - soft
mezzo-forte    mf  - medium loud
forte          f   - loud
fortissimo     ff  - very loud
pianississimo  ppp - very very soft


poco          - a little
gliss         - glissando
T.R.          - toute resonance - let it reverberate
eco           - like an echo
deciso        - with determination
l.v.          - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
dolce         - sweetly, gently
fluido        - in a flowing, smooth style
port          - portamento - slow and regular shift between 2 notes,
                often played on the same string
                NB: the 2nd note is plucked again, in opposition
                with the glissando
con tenerezza - with tenderness
cresc         - crescendo - becoming louder
tr            - trills
arp           - arpeggiate
poco a poco   - little by little
secco         - short and very staccato, sharp, brusque
molto         - much, very
dedillo       - fast up and down strokes with the
                index finger
sempre        - always, still, continuing
sub           - subito - suddenly
sfz           - sforzando - strong, sudden accent on a note or chord
con fuoco     - fierily, with passionate fervor, impetuously
quasi niente  - fade the music away to little more than a bare whisper
Come prima    - in the same manner as the first time
lontano       - from a distance, eerie
Vigoroso      - with emphasis, with vigour
sim           - in a similar manner
pizz          -  muted form of plucking, with a shorter sustain
                (short, percussive effect rather than sustained)
plp           - pulp,flesh - playing bass notes with
                only the flesh of the thumb, no nail
ord           - ordinario - return to the usual way of performing
                after a passage that was indicated with
                a special performance technique
lentissimo    - at a very slow tempo


>       - accented, marked, stressed
h       - hammer-on
p       - pull-off
/       - slide up
\       - slide down
g       - grace note
^       - strumming downwards
v       - strumming upwards
<12>    - natural harmonic on the 12th fret
<19>    - natural harmonic on the 19th fret
<24>    - natural harmonic on the 24th fret
x       - tambour / tambora - 1st string is played by hitting it
          near the bridge with a flat part of the hand
          (this technique is usually done with the side of
          the outstretched thumb)

|--3--| - triplet  - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
