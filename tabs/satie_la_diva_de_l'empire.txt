Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
"This Intermezzo Americain was composed for Pauline Darty, who was known
as 'The Queen of the Slow Waltz' in early 20th Century Paris. It was first
performed about 1904 and subsequently published in 1919." - Gregg Nestor

The Xuefei Yang reference video is Gregg Nestor's arrangement for voice and
solo guitar, and is the closest to the tab that seems to be available.

left hand fingering (LHF)
 1  - index finger
 2  - middle finger
 3  - ring finger
[3  - use the 3rd finger for the barre for the harmonics (measure 16 & 52)
 4  - little finger

c1  - part barre on the 1st fret
C1  - barre on the 1st fret
c2  - part barre on the 2nd fret
C2  - barre on the 2nd fret [etc]

forte         f   - loud
piano         p   - soft
mezzo-forte   mf  - medium loud


leger  - light, relaxed
arp.   - arpeggiate
sub.   - subito - suddenly
rasg.  - rasgueado
gliss. - glissando
sf     - sforzando - strong, sudden accent on a note or a chord

h      - hammer-on
p      - pull-off
/      - slide up
s      - staccato - detached, cut short, note not held for full value
< < <  - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >  - decrescendo - becoming softer
g      - grace note (ornament) - extra note played just before the main note
>      - accented, marked, stressed

<12>  - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
