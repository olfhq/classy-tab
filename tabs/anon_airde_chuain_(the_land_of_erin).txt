Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinky)

CII     barre on the 2nd fret
CVII    barre on the 7th fret
CIX     barre on the 9th fret

piano         p   - soft
mezzo forte   mf  - medium loud
mezzo piano   mp  - medium soft
pianissimo    pp  - very soft

rubato  - expressive and rhythmic freedom (temporary disregard strict tempo)
l.v.    - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
sempre  - always, throughout the piece
arp.    - arpeggiate
cresc.  - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
dim.    - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
molto   - much, very
sub.    - subito - suddenly
F.      - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
Nat.    - natural, discontinue a special effect
niente  - fade the music away to little more than a bare whisper

> > >  - (below the tab) decrescendo - becoming quieter, growing softer
h      - (between the notes) hammer-on
p      - (between the notes) pull-off
>      - accented, marked, stressed
^      - strumming from the lowest note to the highest note
v      - strumming from the highest note to the lowest note

<7>      - natural (open) harmonic on the 7th fret
<12>     - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
5[17]    - artificial harmonic, play 5th fret, point to the 17th fret
7[19]    - artificial harmonic, play 7th fret, point to the 19th fret
