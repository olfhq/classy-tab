Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The first four measures are repeated in the Gabriel Bianco reference video.

left hand fingering
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CI      barre on the 1st fret
CII     barre on the 2nd fret
CIII    barre on the 3rd fret
CV      barre on the 5th fret
CVI     barre on the 6th fret
CVIII   barre on the 8th fret

fortissimo    ff - very loud
forte         f  - loud
piano         p  - soft
sforzando     sf - suddenly louder

dolce    - sweetly
arp      - arpeggiate
cresc.   - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
F.       - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra note played just before the main note
