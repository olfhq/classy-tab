Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
"I treated the guitar first in the manner of the lute (as it had been in the
time of Bach) with a Chaconne and a Prelude, then in the romantic way (as it
had been in the time of Schubert) with three Waltzes, and finally in the modern
way (jazz type), with a Fox-trot..." - Mario Castelnuovo-Tedesco

left hand fingering (LHF)      right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger               p - thumb
2 - middle finger              i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                m - middle finger
4 - little finger              a - ring finger (annular)

CI      barre on the 1st fret
CII     barre on the 2nd fret
CIII    barre on the 3rd fret
CIV     barre on the 4th fret
CVI     barre on the 6th fret
CVII    barre on the 7th fret
CX      barre on the 10th fret

pp          - pianissimo - very soft
p           - piano - soft
mf          - mezzo forte - medium loud
f           - forte - loud
cresc.      - crescendo - becoming louder
decresc.    - decrescendo - becoming softer

espr.       - espressivo - expressively
dim.        - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
un poco     - a little
tratt.      - trattenuto - held back with a sustained quality
F.          - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
a piacere   - at the pleasure of the performer, freely
molto       - much, very
Tempo i     - return to the original tempo
arp.        - arpeggiate
piu lento   - slower
malinconico - melancholy, gloomy
poco mosso  - slower tempo, diminished motion

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
t     - tenuto - note(s) played firmly, held for at least its full length
/     - slide up

|-3-| - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
