From: cso@cth.u-psud.fr (Equipe CSO)

Retabulated by correction of 'Jurgen's' post. Looking at the original piano
score I noticed some disparities between it and Jurgen's tabulation.  I have
therefore attempted to correct it. Also, the low E must be tuned down to D.
There may well be alternative positions that can be used so please don't
take what I've written as law!!!  Experiment... Have fun.

- David Atkinson (email: kaum0@central.susx.ac.uk).

It is Jurgen's from here on:

"Prelude #1 was originally written as an exercise for Bach's students and
is probably one of the most famous preludes from "The Well-Tempered
Clavier". Every classically-trained pianist knows it, but it is
also a beautiful piece for guitar. It consists of a series of arpeggio's
and it should be played as smoothly as possible. Every measure must be
played twice, except for the last three.
I jotted down this transcription a few years ago. I think it was in a
guitar-magazine in 1986 or 1987 or so and I didn't write down the names
of the transcriber and the magazine, so I hope I don't violate copyright-
laws with this posting. Well, here it comes:"

Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)       right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger                p - thumb
2 - middle finger               i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                 m - middle finger
4 - little finger               a - ring finger (annular)

CII    barre on the 2nd fret
CIII   barre on the 3rd fret
CIV    barre on the 4th fret
CV     barre on the 5th fret
CVII   barre on the 7th fret
CIX    barre on the 9th fret
CXI    barre on the 11th fret

h    - hammer-on
/    - slide up
F.   - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

<12>   - natural harmonic on the 12th fret
