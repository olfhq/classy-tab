Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CII    barre on the 2nd fret

piano          p   - soft
mezzo forte    mf  - medium loud
forte          f   - loud
mezzo piano    mp  - medium soft

Lento        - at a slow tempo
ord.         - ordinario - return to the usual way of playing
l.v.         - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
F.           - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
legato       - notes tied together, they should be played in a smooth manner,
               without leaving any space between them
arp.         - arpeggiate
cresc.       - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
accell.      - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
Tempo libero - freely, in free time
allarg.      - allargando - gradually slower and more stately, broaden the tempo
Tempo        - speed (Tempo I - return to the original tempo)
a tempo      - at normal speed, return to the earlier tempo
sostenuto    - at a slower but sustained tempo, with each note
               held for its full value
metalique    - sul ponticello - near the bridge
ad lib       - ad libitum - improvise freely
rall.        - rallentando - slowing down

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
        before the main notes

|-3-| - triplet    - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
|-6-| - sextuplet  - 6 notes played in the time of 4 notes

<12>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
