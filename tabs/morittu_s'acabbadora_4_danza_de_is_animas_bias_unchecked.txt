Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)    right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger             p - thumb
2 - middle finger            i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger              m - middle finger
4 - little finger            a - ring finger (annular)  

mezzo piano    mp  - medium soft
mezzo forte    mf  - medium loud
pianissimo     pp  - very soft
fortissimo     ff  - very loud
piano          p   - soft
forte          f   - loud

bpm     - beats per minute (quarter notes)
Coda    - passage that brings the movement to an end
arp     - arpeggiate
tamb    - tambour / tambora - the strings are played all at once by hitting them
          near the bridge with a flat part of the hand (usually with the side of
          the outstretched thumb)
gliss   - glissando
rit     - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
F.      - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

h  - hammer-on
p  - pull-off
/  - slide up
\  - slide down
g  - grace note (ornament) - extra note played just before the main note
>  - accented, marked, stressed
v  - strumming downwards

<x>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret
