arp.             - arpeggiate

                   (traditionally played - ami)
dim.             - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
pizz.            - muted form of plucking, with a shorter sustain - (place
                   the right side, the side opposite the thumb, of palm of
                   the right hand on the bridge, slightly resting on the
                   bridge, pluck the strings with the thumb or fingers)
poco             - a little (poco rit. - slowing down a little)
rit.             - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
        before the main notes
