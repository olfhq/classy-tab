Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CI       barre on the 1st fret
CVIII    barre on the 8th fret
CIX      barre on the 9th fret

mp espr      - mezzo-piano espressivo - medium soft, expressively
f slancio    - forte slancio          - loud, with enthusiasm, with passion
ff maestoso  - fortissimo maestosto   - very loud, in a majestic, stately manner

Rubato   - expressive and rhythmic freedom
F.       - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
l.v.     - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring, don't dampen the sound
molto    - much, very
rit      - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
sempre   - always, continuously,
Fine     - end, end of the piece
a tempo  - at normal speed
Tempo I  - return to the original tempo

h   - hammer-on
p   - pull-off
/   - slide up
\   - slide down
g   - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
      before the main notes

<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret
