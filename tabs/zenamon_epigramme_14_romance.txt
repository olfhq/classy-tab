Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The tempo of the MIDI file is 96 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).
The order of play is 1-29, 1-16, 30-32.

measure 4 - the sheet has a G (1st string, 3rd fret) on the 1st beat

left hand fingering (LHF) below the tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinky)

===    - note held, length of note, duration
'      - slight pause, breath mark
+      - Coda symbol - end of measure 16: jump to the Coda (measure 30)
arp.   - arpeggiate - rippple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
D.C.   - Da Capo - return to the start (measure 1)
Coda   - end section of a piece (al Coda - play until the Coda symbol)
< < <  - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >  - decrescendo - becoming softer
F.     - Fermata - pause, notes or rests held for longer than their value
g      - grace note (ornament) - extra note played just before the main note
mf     - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
poco   - little, a little (poco rall. - a little slower)
rall.  - rallentando - slowing down (sometimes becoming gradually softer)
