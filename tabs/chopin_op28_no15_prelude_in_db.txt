"Raindrop" Prelude Op. 28 No. 15 (1838)
Frederic Chopin (1810-1849)
Francisco Tarrega arrangement - edition: Boceta, VLlyB 1113 (Barcelona)
tab by Martin Jagos on 2025-05-05 (daedelus2005@seznam.cz)
                                  (martinjagos00@gmail.com)

One of the most beautiful and emotional pieces ever written. Written on a
rainy day in winter 1838 in Valldemossa, Mallorca where Chopin was staying
with his partner George Sand to try and recover from an ilness and to flee
from damp Paris weather, only to find that Mallorca would not be any better.
That winter in Mallorca was very wet and the monastery he was staying at
at allowed the wind and rain in, so his health deteriorated. It was inspired
by the sound of dripping raindrops, which are present througout the piece in
the form of repeated single notes (mainly E note in this arrangement). It is
the longest of the 24 preludes and very much reflects Chopin's ill and
desperate mood around this period of his life.

Francisco Tarrega's arrangement can be viewed as the best sounding and most
playable arrangement and the best compromise between Ohdan's classtab tab,
Youtube's low-fret arrangements and Classclef's nice-but-high-fret version.

About the piece:
https://www.wnyc.org/story/72008-the-fishko-files-chopins-raindrop-prelude/

Tablature legend:
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
/  - slide up
\  - slide down
F. - fermata - pause, at the end of piece - sustain/hold
p  - piano - soft or quiet
c5 - part bare
C9 - full barre
ff - fortissimo - very loud
cres. - crescendo - gradually louder
smorzando - fade away gradually
slentando - gradually slowing down
dim. - diminuendo - gradually softer
poco piu animato - a little more lively
sostenuto con expresione - play with sustained & prolonged notes or phrases to
                           give it sense of continuity and expression
