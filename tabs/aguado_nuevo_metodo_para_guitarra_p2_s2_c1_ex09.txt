Notes & Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The tab is based on the Op 6 (Madrid, 1840) edition.

Aguado wrote six 'Methods' often recycling the same lessons and exercises.
For info on three of the methods see - "Dionisio Aguado - New Guitar Method"
Introduction to the Tecla edition (Brian Jeffery, 1981)
https://www.tecla.com/extras/0001/0011/0011intro.htm

The tempo of the MIDI is 112 bpm (beats per minute - eighth notes).
The order of play is measures 1-8, 1-7, 9-17, 10-17.

measure 6 - the 1843 edition has D-A#-F# for the chord (not D#-A-f#)
measure 7 - the 1843 edition has G (6th string 3rd fret) in the bass
            (not B) - Norbert Neunzling plays G (which sounds ok)

in the 1843 edition, measures 10,12,14,15,16 are played differently -
