Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Norbert Neunzling plays this piece immediately following the Trio in C (No 5).
After measure 16 of No 6 Norbert returns to measure 1 of No 5, playing
straight through the Trio without repeats, and ending at measure 16. Though
they are numbered as separate lessons, this is what is indicated in the sheet.

The tempo of the Norbert Neunzling vid and the MIDI is 96 bpm (quarter notes).
