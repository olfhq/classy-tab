Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
This collection of studies is intended for the intermediate guitarist.
The order of play is measures 1-32, 1-15, 33-65, 50-63, 66-68, 1-32, 69-91.
The starting tempo of the MIDI is 144 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).

right hand fingering (RHF) below the tab
p - thumb
i - index finger (forefinger)
m - middle finger
a - ring finger (annular)

===    - note held, length of note, duration
+      - Coda symbol (al + - continue until the Coda symbol
$      - sign
Coda   - the end section of a piece
D.S.   - return to the sign (al $2 - continue until the second sign)
F.     - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
h      - hammer-on
L.V.   - Laissez Vibrer - let the notes continue to sound
molto  - much, a lot (rit. molto - slowing right down)
p      - (between the notes) pull-off
rit.   - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
<7>    - natural (open) harmonic on the 7th fret
<12>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret

repeat section      1___ play the 1st time      2_______  play on repeat
