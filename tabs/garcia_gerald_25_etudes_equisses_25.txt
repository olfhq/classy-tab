Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
This collection of studies is intended for the intermediate guitarist.
The tempo of the MIDI is 108 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).
The lengths of the notes are taken from the sheet, but these are not
always held for their full length in the reference videos.

measure 11 - in the sheet the G (open 3rd string) is a half note (not
             a dotted quarter note)

===     - length of note, duration, note held
>       - accented, marked, stressed
/       - slide the note up
\       - slide the note down
< < <   - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >   - decresccendo - becoming softer
accel.  - accellerando - becoming faster
arp.    - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
calmo   - calm, calmly (piu calmo
cresc.  - crescendo - becoming louder
f       - forte - loud, strong
F.      - Fermata - pause - note held for longer than its value
ff      - fortissimo - very loud
g       - grace note (ornament) - extra note(s) played before the main note -
          following note(s) usually played as a hammer-on, pull-off or slide -
          on guitar it's often easier to play grace notes at the same time as
          any accompanying bass notes (on the beat rather than just before it)
molto   - much, very (molto cresc. - becoming much louder)
p       - (below the tab) piano - soft, quietly
p       - (between the notes) pull-off
piu     - more (piu calmo - more calmly, a little slower)
poco    - little (poco rit. - slowing down a little)
                 (poco accel. - speeding up a little)
pp      - pianissimo - very soft
nat.    - natural - return to the natural way of playing
pizz.   - pizzicato - muted (usually played by plucking the string with the
          right thumb and using the palm of the hand to mute the sting)
sempre  - always (sempre pizz. - continue playing pizzicato)
string. - stringendo - rapidly accelerating (sometimes becoming louder)
rasg.   - rasgueado - the strings are strummed with the back of the fingernails
rit.    - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
sfz     - sforzando - suddenly loud, heavily stressed
tempo   - speed (tempo primo - return to the original speed)
ten.    - tenuto - played firmly, note held for at least its full value
