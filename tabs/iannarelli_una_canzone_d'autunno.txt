Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)          right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger                   p - thumb
2 - middle finger                  i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                    m - middle finger
4 - little finger                  a - ring finger (annular)

CI      barre on 1st fret
CIII    barre on 3rd fret
CV      barre on 5th fret
CVII    barre on 7th fret
CIX     barre on 9th fret

Andante      - played fairly slowly, at a walking pace
con moto     - with movement, in a spirited manner
l.v.         - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
m.d.         - right hand
poco         - a little (poco rit. - slowing down a little)
rall.        - rallentando - slowing down
rit          - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
con liberta  - freely
cantando     - in a singing style
F.           - Fermata - pause, notes held
breve        - hold the rest for 8 beats (a long time!)
piu mosso    - more motion, faster
cantando     - in a singing manner
a tempo      - at normal speed
UM           - upper mordent - play the main (melody) note, followed by the
               scale note above, and then return to the original note - (it
               it may be extended, alternating between the two notes)
tr.          - trills
sostenuto    - in a sustained prolonged manner
Tempo primo  - return to the original tempo
piu lento    - a little slower
turn         - ornament - play the scale note above, the main note, the note
               below (usually a semitone below) and finally the main note again
slentando    - gradually decreasing in tempo

h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
'    - breath mark, luftpause

<12>   - natural harmonic on the 12th fret
<17>   - natural harmonic on the 17th fret
<19>   - natural harmonic on the 19th fret
<24>   - natural harmonic on the 24th fret
1[13]  - artificial harmonic, play 1st fret, point to the 13th fret
3[15]  - artificial harmonic, play 3rd fret, point to the 15th fret
5[17]  - artificial harmonic, play 5th fret, point to the 17th fret

|-3-| - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
