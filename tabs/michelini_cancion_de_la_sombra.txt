Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
In the Damiano Mercuri reference video, the last note of measure 21 (B)
and the D in measure 45 are played as octave harmonics on the 12th fret.

left hand fingering
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CII    barre on the 2nd fret
CIII   barre on the 3rd fret
CVI    barre on the 6th fret
CVII   barre on the 7th fret
1/2    part barre
[      barre

Lento       - at a slow tempo
arp.        - arpeggiate
dolce       - gently, sweetly
F.          - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
rit.        - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
espr.       - expressively
rall.       - rallentando - slowing down
molto       - much, very
libero      - in a free, unrestrained style
vib.        - vibrato
Tempo I     - return to the original tempo
Piu lento   - slower
m.d.        - with the right hand

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off

x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret

|-3-| - triplet    - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
|-5-| - quintuplet - 5 notes played in the time of 4 notes
