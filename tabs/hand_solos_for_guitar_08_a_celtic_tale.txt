Notes and Legend

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CIII    barre on the 3rd fret

poco   - a little
rubato - expressive and rhythmic freedom
arp.   - arpeggiate
F.     - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
lunga  - (lunga F.) it emphasizes a significantly longer hold
sfz    - sforzando - strong, sudden accent on a note or chord

h      - hammer-on
p      - pull-off
/      - slide up
\      - slide down
|-3-|  - triplet - 3 notes in the time of 2 notes
g      - grace note (ornament) - extra note(s) played just before the main note,
         usually as hammer-ons, pull-offs or slides

<7>    - natural (open) harmonic on the 7th fret
<12>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
