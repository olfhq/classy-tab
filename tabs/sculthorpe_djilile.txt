Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
"This work was originally a piano piece, written in 1986. It was arranged for
guitar by a Canadian friend, the composer and guitarist Steve Wingfield. The
opening melody, on which the work is based, is a transcription of a chant from
Arnhem Land, in northern Australia, collected in the late 1950s by Professor
A.P. Elkin. The title 'Djilile' means 'whistling-duck on a billabong'. The
first performance was given by John Williams at the Hong Kong Cultural Centre,
on 5 October 2003." - Peter Sculthorpe

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CI      barre on the 1st fret
CIII    barre on the 3rd fret
CVIII   barre on the 8th fret
[       barre with the index finger

piano          p   - soft
mezzo piano    mp  - medium soft
forte          f   - loud
mezzo forte    mf  - medium loud

molto        - much, very
sostenuto    - in a sustained, prolonged manner
l.v.         - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
poco rall.   - poco rallentando - slowing down a little
a tempo      - at normal speed
cresc.       - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
dim          - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
meno mosso   - 'with less motion', at a slower tempo
poco a poco  - little by little
tempo primo  - return to the original tempo
arp.         - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from lowest note to highest note
F.           - Fermata - pause before continuing

g  - grace note (ornament) - extra note played just before the main note

<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
