Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

forte         f    - loud
piano         p    - soft
mezzo piano   mp   - medium soft
mezzo forte   mf   - medium loud
fortissimo    ff   - very loud

CIV    barre on 4th fret
CIX    barre on 9th fret

bpm         - beats per minute (quarter notes)
rianimando  - reanimating
sub.        - subito - suddenly
tamb .      - tambour / tambora - the strings are played all at once by
              hitting them near the bridge with a flat part of the hand
              (usually with the side of the outstretched thumb)
m.s.        - mano sinistra - hammer the open strings with the left hand

<12>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret

|-3-|  - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
