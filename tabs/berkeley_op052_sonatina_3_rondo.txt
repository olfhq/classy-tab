Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The tab is based on the Chester 1958 edition, incorporating corrections from
David Harvey's article, affecting measures 4,19,20,23,27,62,68,79,133
https://davethehat.medium.com/lennox-berkeleys-sonatina-for-guitar-e83be43104b7

measure 67 - Julian Bream doesn't play the upper note (B) of the 1st interval -
             David Harvey suggests playing the 1st interval (D-B) as harmonics
             (which would allow the upper note to continue sounding)

measure 133 - E# on 3rd string 10th fret (not E) sounds better?

measure 136 - Julian Bream plays the interval E-E (adds open E on 1st string)

measure 137 - in the last sheet the last harmonic is 6th string 12th fret

measure 160 - Julian Bream adds F# (3rd string) to the last chord (F#-B-D#-A)

left hand fingering (LHF)     right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger              p - thumb
2 - middle finger             i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger               m - middle finger
4 - little finger             a - ring finger (annular)

cI       barre on the 1st fret
cII      barre on the 2nd fret
cIII     barre on the 3rd fret
cIV      barre on the 4th fret
cV       barre on the 5th fret
cVI      barre on the 6th fret
cVII     barre on the 7th fret
cVIII    barre on the 8th fret
cIX      barre on the 9th fret
[        barre with the index finger

forte         f   - loud
piano         p   - soft
fortissimo    ff  - very loud
mezzo-forte   mf  - medium loud

arp.     - arpeggiate
cresc    - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
F.       - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
dim.     - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
a tempo  - return to the original tempo
piu      - more
accel.   - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
> > >    - crescendo - becoming gradually louder

h   - hammer-on
p   - pull-off
/   - slide up
\   - slide down
^   - strumming downwards
v   - strumming upwards
>   - accented, marked, stressed

<x> - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret

|-3-| - triplet   - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
|-6-| - sextuplet - 6 notes played in the time of 4 notes
