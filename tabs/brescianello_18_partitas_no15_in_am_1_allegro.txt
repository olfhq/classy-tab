Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Bradford Werner suggests this piece is suitable for intermediate guitarists,
especially if the hammer-ons and pull-offs are left out (as some of them are
difficult to play evenly at a fast tempo).

The tepo of the MIDI is 100 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).

measure 37 - in the sheet the bottom note of the 1st chord is G (not G#)

===    - length of note, note held, duration
|~6~|  - sextuplet - 6 notes in the time of 4 notes
|~7~|  - septuplet - seven notes in the time of 4 notes
#      - ornament (use trills, mordents etc)
h      - hammer-on
p      - pull-off
