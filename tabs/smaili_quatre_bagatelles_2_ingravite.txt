Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger )forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinkie)

CII    barre on 2nd fret
CIV    barre on 4th fret

mezzo piano    mp - medium soft
forte          f  - loud
piano          p  - soft
mezzo forte    mf - medium loud
pianissimo     pp - very soft
fortissimo     ff - very loud

ad lib.    - ad libitum - improvise freely
rit.       - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
calando    - gradual decrease in both the tempo and volume
poco       - a little
sul pont.  - sul ponticello - near the bridge
arp.       - arpeggiate
a tempo    - at normal speed
tamb.      - tambora - the strings are played all at once by hitting them
             near the bridge with a flat part of the hand (usually with the side of
             the outstretched thumb)
loco       - back to playing notes at their normal pitch, it cancels a previous octave
F.         - Fermata - pause, notes held
gliss      - glissando

>     - accented, marked, stressed
h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - (LHF) slide the finger up to the next note
\     - (LHF) slide the finger down to the the next note
<5>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 5th fret
<7>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 7th fret
<12>  - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
