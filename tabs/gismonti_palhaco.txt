Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Sergio Assad has not published his solo guitar arrangement of Palhaco, so
there will probably be noticeable differences in the way it is performed.
The three reference videos are all in the key of A major, but the guitar
is tuned one semi-tone lower in the Julie Malia recording, so it sounds
like Ab major. Allen Mathews and Wojtek Niemotko both play the opening
theme on the high frets; in the tab it is played on on the low frets.

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

C(n)   barre on the nth fret
c(n)   part barre on the nth fret
[      barre with the index finger

h  - hammer-on
p  - pull-off
/  - slide up
\  - slide down
g  - grace note (ornament) - an extra note played before the main note -
     the next note is usually played as a hammer-on, pull-off or slide

<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret

|-3-| - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
