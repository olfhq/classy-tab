Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The piece is written for a five string guitar (no bottom string).
The tempo of the MIDI is 84 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).
The order of play is measures 1-19, 1-19, 20-58, 20-58.

                       ~

             |-5p3p1h3-3h5p3---------1-3-|
             |---------------------------|
             |---------------------------|
             |-0=======------------------|
             |---------------------------|

===    - length of note, note held, duration
~      - ornament (on the upper note) - played as upper mordents in the Norbert
         Neunzling reference video, hammering-on the scale note above then
         pulling-off to return to the original note
|-3-|  - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
g      - grace note (ornament) - an extra note played immediately before the
         main note, here played as a pull-off
h      - hammer-on
p      - pull-off
