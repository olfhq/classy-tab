Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QZ0of6lobRM - Laura Mazon Franqui
video: https://youtu.be/HclyHtsdTpw?t=410 - Silvia Nogales Barrios (from 6m 50s)
video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BfsL1HarflA - Karla Beltran (live)
video: https://youtu.be/wcXxc-mfMyk?t=369 - Maria Guzman (live) (from 6m 9s)

The starting tempo in the sheet is 100 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes),
but the reference videos are closer to 200 bpm. The MIDI tempo is 176 bpm.

measures 66,68,72 - Silvia Nogales Barrios plays F for the 1st note (not F#)
measures 75-76 - not played by Silvia Nogales Barrios
measure 85 - the sheet has a closed repeat sign at the end of the measure but
             there is no eqivalent open repeat sign preceding it - Antonia
             Haslinger and Laura Mazon Franqui ignore it - Silvia Nogales
             Barrios repeats from measure 80
measures 86-93 - the sheet shows the chords as slides down from the 1st chord
                 in each measure but they are played separately in the vids
measures 91,93 - in the sheet the chords are C-F#-F#-C, B-B-E-B, A-B-E-A but
                 in the vids they are played C-F-C, B-B-E-B, A-B-D-A
measures 94-86 - in the sheet the chords are just arpeggiated, but in all the
                 reference videos they are strummed alternatively up and down

Left Hand Fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger (pinkie)

=====   - note held, length of note, duration
= = =   - allow the note to continue ringing
>       - accented, marked, stressed
/       - (between the notes) slide the note up
\       - (between the notes) slide the note down
/       - (below the tab) slide the finger up to play the next note
\       - (below the tab) slide the finger down to play the next note
> > >   - decrescendo - becoming softer
arp.    - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
C2      - barre on the 2nd fret
C5      - barre on the 5th fret
c7        part barre on the 7th fret
f       - forte - loud, strong
F.      - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
p       - piano - soft, quietly
pizz.   - Bartok pizzicato - the note is plucked so it snaps on the fretboard
rall.   - rallentando - slowing down (and becoming softer)
ten.    - played firmly, note held for at least its full value
