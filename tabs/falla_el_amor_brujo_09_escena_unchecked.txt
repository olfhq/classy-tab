Fermata   - pause, notes held for longer than their value

h   - hammer-on
p   - pull-off
>   - accented, marked, stressed
^   - strumming downwards
v   - strumming upwards

<12>  - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret

|-3-| - triplet    - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
|-5-| - quintuplet - 5 notes played in the time of 4 notes
