Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
measures 20-23 - in the Flavio Cucchi video the first chords of m21 & m22 are
played with a grace note (as in the tab) and sound arpeggiated - however the
grace notes do not appear in the sheeet but are shown as part of the chord -
in the Segovia audio recording the chord in m22 isn't arpeggiated - so some or
all of these four measures can presumably be played on the higher frets

measures 29,31 - in the sheet the chord on the 1st beat is G-C-Eb-A, which
could be played on the bottom 4 strings (with difficulty, unless you're Segovia)
- however m29,30 are the same as m65,66, except that the C is missing from the
1st chord of measure 65, so perhaps it can be omitted

3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

piano           p    - soft

mezzo forte     mf   - medium loud
pianississimo   ppp  - extremely soft
