"'Guantanamera' (Spanish for 'The woman from Guantanamo')is a Cuban patriotic
song, which uses a poem by the Cuban poet Jose Marti for the lyrics. The
official writing credits have been given to Joseito Fernandez, who first
popularized the song on radio as early as 1929..." - Wikipedia
