Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Johan Smith doesn't repeat measures 1-16.

left hand fingering (LHF)     right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger              p - thumb
2 - middle finger             i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger               m - middle finger
4 - little finger             a - ring finger (annular)

CIII     barre on the 3rd fret
CV       barre on the 5th fret
CVII     barre on the 7th fret
CVIII    barre on the 8th fret

forte          f   - loud
mezzo-piano    mp  - medium soft
fortissimo     ff  - very loud
mezzo-forte    mf  - medium loud

Allegro        - quickly and brightly
arp.           - arpeggiate
F              - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
rall.          - rallentando - gradually slowing down
a tempo        - at normal speed
poco piu mosso - 'a little more motion', a little faster
cresc.         - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
meno mosso     - less rapidly, slower
pesante        - heavy and ponderous

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - slide up
p^    - strumming downwards with the thumb
>     - accented, marked, stressed

|-5-| - quintuplet - 5 notes played in the time of 4 notes
