Performance Notes
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
* In measure 6, the sheet music keeps the hand in first position. However, I
  feel this cuts off the sound too sharply, so I play it with a barre on fret V.

===    held note
p      pull-off

mf     mezzo forte (medium loud)
mp     mezzo piano (medium soft)
p      piano (soft)
pp     pianissimo (very soft)

accompagnando    accompaniment
canto il basso   melody in the bass part
eco              like an echo
l.v.             let ring
sempre legato    always legato

%               segno (sign)
dal % al Fine   return to % and play until Fine

Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)      right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger               p - thumb
2 - middle finger              i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                m - middle finger
4 - little finger              a - ring finger (annular)

CI    barre on the 1st fret

mezzo piano     mp  - medium soft
piano           p   - soft
pianissimo      pp  - very soft
mezzo forte     mf  - medium loud

Lento assai    - very slowly
l.v.           - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
eco            - like an echo
canta il basso - emphasize the bass notes
dal % al FINE  - from % to FINE (measures 5-10) (end at FINE)
