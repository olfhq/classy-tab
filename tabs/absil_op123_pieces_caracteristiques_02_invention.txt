Notes & Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The MIDI tempo is 76 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).
This is a two part invention, reflected in the LHF below the tab.

measures 10,28 - in the sheet the barre is shown as being held until the end of
                 the measure but the A on the 3rd string is only an eighth note

left hand fingering (LHF) below tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinky)

===    - notes held, length of note, duration
> > >  - decrescendo, becoming softer
/      - (LHF) slide the finger up to be in position to play the next note
\      - (LHF) slide the finger down to be in position to play the next note
C2     - barre on the 2nd fret
f      - forte - loud, strong
F.     - Fermata - pause, note(s) held for longer than their value
mf     - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
p      - piano - quiet, softly
rit.   - slowing down, suddenly slower
