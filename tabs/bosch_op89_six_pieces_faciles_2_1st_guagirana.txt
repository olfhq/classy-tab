Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
'Guagirana' may be a different spelling of guajira, a Cuban peasant dance/song.
The MIDI tempo is 76 bpm (beats per minute - dotted half notes).
The order of play is measures 1-16, 1-15, 17, 18-32, 18-32, 33-57.

measures 34, 35 - the D (3rd fret) and B (open) on the 2nd string are optional
measures 40,41 - the left hand fingering follows the video (not the sheet)

left hand fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinky)

===    - note held, length of note, duration

mf     - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
