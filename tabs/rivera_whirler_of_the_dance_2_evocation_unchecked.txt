Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

[    barre with the index finger

mezzo-forte    mf  - medium loud
mezzo-piano    mp  - medium soft
piano          p   - soft
forte          f   - loud

rit      - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
A tempo  - at normal speed
accel    - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
rubato   - expressive and rhythmic freedom
F.       - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

h   - hammer-on
p   - pull-off
>   - accented, marked, stressed

<12>  - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret

|-3-| - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
