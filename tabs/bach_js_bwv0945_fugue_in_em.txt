Fugue in E Minor, BWV 945 - Johann Sebastian Bach (1685-1750)
Tab by Albertto Becker Bulsara
beato_pex@hotmail.com

Bach's authorship of this work is doubtful; it may possibly have
been composed by Christoph Graupner (1683-1760).
This adaptation for guitar preserves the original key. Try to hold
the notes and chords for as long as possible.
The piece is somewhat fast, between 70 and 80 bpm.

Tuning: eBGDAE          Key: E minor         Time: 4/4
