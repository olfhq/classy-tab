Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
A variant of an early 19th century song from Moravia and Lower Austria about
the Hunchback Mannlei (Hunchbacked Little Man).

The tempo of the the MIDI is 92 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).
The order of play is 0-20, 5-12, 21-26.

measure 18 - in the sheet the 2nd chord is B-E-G-D (not B-D-G-D)

left hand fingering (LHF) below tab      right hand fingering (RHF) above tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)            p - thumb
2 - middle finger                        i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger (annular)                m - middle finger
4 - little finger (pinky)                a - ring finger (annular)

===    - note held, length of note, duration
>      - accented, marked, stressed
$      - segno - sign
+      - Coda symbol
> > >  - decrescendo - growing softer
al +   - play until the Coda symbol (end of measure 12) then jump to the Coda
Coda   - the end section of a piece (starts at measure 21)
Dal $  - Dal Segno - return to the sign (start of measure 5)
f      - forte - strong, loudly
ff     - fortissimo - very louid
pp     - pianissimo - very soft
s      - staccato - detached, cut off, notes not held for their full value
