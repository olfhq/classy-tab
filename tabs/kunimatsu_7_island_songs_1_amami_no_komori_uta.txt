left hand fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinky)

===   - note held, length of note, duration
/     = slide the finger up to the next note
bpm   - beats per minute (quarter notes)
c7    - part barre on the 7th fret
C7    - barre on the 7th fret
F.    - Fermata - pause, notes hold for longer than their value
h     - hammer-on
mf    - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
mp    - mezzo-piano - moderately soft, fairly quiet
rit.  - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
t     - tenuto - notes played firmly, held for at least their full value
<7>   - natural (open) harmonic on the 7th fret
<12>  - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
