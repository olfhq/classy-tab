Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)      right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger               p - thumb
2 - middle finger              i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                m - middle finger
4 - little finger              a - ring finger (annular)

CI      barre on the 1st fret
CII     barre on the 2nd fret
CIII    barre on the 3rd fret
CIV     barre on the 4th fret
CV      barre on the 5th fret
CVII    barre on the 7th fret

mezzo-piano    mp  - medium soft
pianissimo     pp  - very soft
mezzo-forte    mf  - medium loud
forte          f   - loud
piano          p   - soft

bpm         - beats per minute (quarter notes)
dolce       - gently, sweetly
gliss       - glissando
arp         - arpeggiate
r.h.        - play with the right hand
port        - portamento - slow and regular shift between 2 notes,
              often played on the same string
              NB: the 2nd note is plucked again (as opposed to the glissando)
a tempo     - at normal speed
plp         - pulp, flesh - playing bass notes with
              only the flesh of the thumb, no nail
tr          - trills
comodo      - in a comfortable tempo
rit         - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
molto       - much, very
poco a poco - little by little
espress     - expressively
l.h.        - play with the left hand
legato      - smoothly, with no gaps between the notes
meno        - less
rall        - rallentando - slowing down
piu         - more
allarg      - allargando - gradually slower and more stately
eco         - like an echo
cantando    - in a singing style
stringendo  - gradually faster
accel       - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
pesante     - in a heavy, ponderous manner
sub         - subito - suddenly
a piacere   - 'at pleasure', ad libitum, freely
Come prima  - in the same manner as the first time
sfz         - sforzando - strong, sudden accent on a note or chord
lento       - at a slow tempo
sempre      - aleays, still, continuing
ritard      - ritardanto - gradually decrease the tempo
Lentissimo  - at a very slow tempo
metal.      - metalique (ponticello) - pluck the strings close to the bridge
lontano     - extremely quietly, as from far away
F.          - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - slide up
\     - slide down
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
        before the main notes
>     - accented, marked, stressed

<x>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret

|-3-| - triplet  - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
