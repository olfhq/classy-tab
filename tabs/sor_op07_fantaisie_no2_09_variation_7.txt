Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The numbering of the variations in the sheet is confusing, going straight from
VII to VIII in both editions with the following section of the piece only being
named "Finale" in the Stich und Druck edition. In the tabs Variation VIII
has been renumbered Variation 7 and the Finale has been tabbed separately.

Where there are difficult stretches, you can release the chords early or play
the note on a different string - it was written for a smaller guitar!

The tempo of the MIDI is 58 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).

===  - note held, length of note, duration
g    - grace note (ornament) - appoggiatura (passing note) - an extra note
       played before the main note (here played as a pull-off)
