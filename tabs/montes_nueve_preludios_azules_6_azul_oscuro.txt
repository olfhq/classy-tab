Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The tempo of the MIDI is 112 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes). Alfonso
Montes plays it at 120 bpm in the reference video, and doesn't play measure 79.

Note the rhythm change in m.32-37 & m.40-41, from 4+4 (eighth notes) to 3+3+2.

measures 18,56 - in the Alfonso Montes recording the last note is C (8th fret)

measure 19 - Alfonso Montes plays the last chord as eighth notes (continuing
             immediately with the next measure)

left hand fingering (LHF) below tab      right hand fingering (RHF) above tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)            p - thumb
2 - middle finger                        i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger (annular)                m - middle finger
4 - little finger (pinky)                a - ring finger (annular)

=====    - note held, length of note, duration
/        - glissando - slide the note up or down to the next note allowing
           the intervening notes to be heard
|~3~|    - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
>        - slightly accented
arp.     - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
cresc.   - crescendo - growing louder
decresc. - decrescendo - growing softer
f        - forte - loud, strong
F.       - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
h        - hammer-on
p        - (between the notes) pull-off
pp       - pianissimo - very quiet
rit.     - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
tempo    - speed (a tempo - return to the original speed)

c1       - part barre on the 1st fret
C1       - barre on the 1st fret
c2       - part barre on the 2nd fret
C2       - barre on the 2nd fret [etc]

<12>     - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
2<14>    - artificial octave harmonic - LH 2nd fret, RH points to 14th fret
3<15>    - artificial octave harmonic - LH 3rd fret, RH points to 15th fret
