Notes and Legend

The tempo of the MIDI is 80 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).

===  - length of note, note held, duration
>    - accented, marked, stressed
f    - forte - loud, strong
g    - grace note (ornament) - extra note(s) just before the main note,
       usually played as hammer-ons pull-offs or slides - on a guitar
       it's often easier to play the grace note at the same time as any
       accompanying bass notes, on the beat rather than just before it
h    - hammer-on
p    - (below the tab) piano - soft, quietly
p    - (between the notes) pull-off
