left hand fingering below the tab (LHF)
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinky)

===  - note held, length of note, duration
     - optional note on open string ?
c2   - part barre on the 2nd fret
C2   - barre on the 2nd fret
C5   - barre on the 5th fret
h    - hammer-on
LM   - lower mordent (ornament) - play the main note, quickly followed by
       the note below (in the scale), then quickly back to the original note
p    - pull-off
UM   - lower mordent (ornament) - play the main note, quickly followed by
       the note above (in the scale), then quickly back to the original note
