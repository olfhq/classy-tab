dim               - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer

rubato            - expressive and rhythmic freedom (temporary disregard strict tempo)
ritenendo         - getting slower
legato            - in a smooth flowing manner, without breaks between notes
poco a poco       - little by little
string            - stringendo - gradually faster

espressivo        - expressively
rall              - rallentando - slowing down
