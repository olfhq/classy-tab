Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
"I found a silversmith lying in his straw bed... his hair seemed curly,
like the moth-eaten tow hair of old dolls, which falls out when you run
your hand over it." - Juan Ramon Jimenez

measure 27 - in the Sainz de la Maza collection published by Union Musical
             Editiones (1998) the 1st chord is A-B-C#, but the fingering
             indicates A-G-C#, which is what is played in the reference video
measure 40 - the Fermata (pause) means to wait before continuing

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CI      barre on the 1st fret
CII     barre on the 2nd fret
CIII    barre on the 3rd fret
CV      barre on the 5th fret

mezzo forte    mf - medium loud
piano          p  - soft
forte          f  - loud

bpm        - beats per minute (quarter notes)
arp.       - arpeggiate
eco        - like an echo
sim.       - in a similar manner
ten.       - tenuto - hold, sustain the note/chord to its full value
a tempo    - at normal speed
poco       - a little
rit.       - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
cedendo    - getting slower
meno mosso - at reduced speed
sonoro     - sonorous, resonant
morendo    - fade out
F.         - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

/      - slide up
\      - slide down
>      - accented, marked, stressed
'      - slight pasuse, breath mark
<12>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
|-5-|  - quintuplet - 5 notes played in the time of 4 notes
