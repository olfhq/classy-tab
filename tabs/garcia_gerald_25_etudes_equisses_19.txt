Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
This collection of studies is intended for the intermediate guitarist.
Robert Cronin doesn't repeat measures 25-24.
The starting tempo of the MIDI is 108 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).

left hand fingering (LHF) below tab      right hand fingering (RHF) above tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)            p - thumb
2 - middle finger                        i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger (annular)                m - middle finger
4 - little finger (pinky)                a - ring finger (annular)

===    - note held, length of note, duration
< < <  - (below the tab) crescendo - getting louder
> > >  - (below the tab) decrescendo - becoming softer
arp.   - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
f      - forte - loud, strong
ff     - fortissimo - very loud
F.     - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
mp     - mezzo-piano - moderately soft
p      - (below the tab) piano - soft, quiet
s      - staccato - detached, cut off, note(s) not held for their full value
sfz    - sforzando - strongly accented, suddenly louder
