left hand fingering (LHF)

CI     barre on the 1st fret

mezzo forte    mf  - medium loud
forte          f   - loud
piano          p   - soft

ten.       - tenuto - hold, sustain a note for its full length
rit.       - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
con anima  - with spirit, animatedly
cresc.     - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
decresc.   - decrescendo - becoming gradually softer
allarg.    - allargando - getting slower and broader (more majestic)
Tempo I    - return to the original tempo
D.C. +     - Da Capo - from the beginning and play till + then jump to Coda
Coda       - the end section of the piece
dim.       - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
F.         - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
