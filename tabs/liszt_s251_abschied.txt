Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering         right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger            p - thumb
2 - middle finger           i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger             m - middle finger
4 - little finger           a - ring finger (annular)

CI     barre on the 1st fret
CIII   barre on the 3rd fret
CV     barre on the 5th fret

mezzo forte    mf  - medium loud
pianissimo     pp  - very soft
piano          p   - soft
forte          f   - loud

Andante     - moderately slow
dim.        - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
F.          - Fermata - pause, notes held
un poco     - a little
piu lento   - slower
cresc.      - crescendo - gradually louder
decresc.    - decrescendo - gradually softer
sempre      - always, still, continuing
legato      - notes played smoothly without leaving any space between them
rit.        - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
a tempo     - at normal speed
largamente  - with slowness and breadth
ten.        - tenuto - played firmly, notes held for at least their full length
por.        - portato - stressed and moderately short
perdendosi  - fade out

>   - (above the tab) accented, marked, stressed
g   - grace note (ornament) - extra note played immediately before the main note
=== - notes held for another full measure

<12>   - natural harmonic on the 12th fret
1[13]  - artificial harmonic, play 1st fret, point to the 13th fret
3[15]  - artificial harmonic, play 3rd fret, point to the 15th fret
5[17]  - artificial harmonic, play 5th fret, point to the 17th fret
