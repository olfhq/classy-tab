Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CII     barre on the 2nd fret
CIII    barre on the 3rd fret
CV      barre on the 5th fret
CVII    barre on the 7th fret

forte          f   - loud
mezzo piano    mp  - medium soft
pianissimo     pp  - very soft
mezzo forte    mf  - medium loud

bpm             - beats per minute (half notes)
rall.           - rallentando - slowing down
dim.            - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
a tempo         - at normal speed
meno            - less
leggero         - lightly, gracefully
sost. il tempo  - sostenuto il tempo - at a slower but sustained tempo
molto           - much, very
F.              - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

h      - hammer-on
p      - pull-off
g      - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played just before the main note
/      - slide the note up
st     - staccato - detached, cut off, note not held for full length
< < <  - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >  - decrescendo - becoming softer
