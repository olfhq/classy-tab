mezzo forte     mf  - medium loud
piano           p   - soft
forte           f   - loud
mezzo piano     mp  - medium soft

Tempo libero      - comfortable tempo (moderate speed)
bpm               - beats per minute (quarter notes)
marcato il basso  - emphasize the bass notes
accompagnando     - accompanying (like an echo)
marc.             - marcato - with emphasis
legato            - in a smooth flowing manner
cresc.            - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
sfz               - sforzando - strong, sudden accent on a note or chord
>                 - accented, marked, stressed
F.                - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

< < <  - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >  - decrescendo - becoming softer
