Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
"I treated the guitar first in the manner of the lute (as it had been in the
time of Bach) with a Chaconne and a Prelude, then in the romantic way (as it
had been in the time of Schubert) with three Waltzes, and finally in the modern
way (jazz type), with a Fox-trot..." - Mario Castelnuovo-Tedesco

left hand fingering (LHF)      right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger               p - thumb
2 - middle finger              i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                m - middle finger
4 - little finger              a - ring finger (annular)

CII     barre on the 2nd fret
CIII    barre on the 3rd fret
CV      barre on the 5th fret
CVI     barre on the 6th fret

pianissimo     pp  - very soft
mezzo forte    mf  - medium loud
forte          f   - loud

espr.         - espressivo - expressively
arp.          - arpeggiate

capriccioso   - free and impulsive
tratt.        - trattenuto - held back with a sustained quality
Tempo I       - return to the original tempo
dolce         - gently, sweetly
lontano       - from a distance, as from far away

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off

<12>  - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret
