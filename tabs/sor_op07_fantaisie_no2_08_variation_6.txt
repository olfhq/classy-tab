Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The tempo of the Aaron Haas video is 63 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).
The tempo of the MIDI is 52 bpm.

In the sheet the difference between phrasing and hammer-ons / pull-offs isn't
clear, so use hammer-ons and pull-offs as seems natural and if necessary play
the notes on different strings -- where there are difficult stretches, don't
feel obliged to hang on to the chords - it was written for a smaller guitar!

measure 11 - the 2nd, 3rd, 7th & 8th notes are 64th notes!

measure 16 - Aaron Haas adds a D (2nd string 3rd fret) before the last chord
             ["Hey wait a minute. What did you put that on the end for?"
              "Well it was just a little, you know, embellishment"
              "Oh yeah, You keep that up and the first thing you know
              This record'll swing. And then where will we be?"
