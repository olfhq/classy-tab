left hand fingering        right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger           p - thumb
2 - middle finger          i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger            m - middle finger
4 - little finger          a - ring finger (annular)

CIII   barre on the 3rd fret
CV     barre on the 5th fret

mezzo piano    mp - medium soft
piano          p  - piano
mezzo forte    mf - medium loud
forte          f  - loud
fortissimo     ff - very loud

Assez lent        - quite slow, rather slow
libre de mouv.    - freely
Un peu moins lent - 'a little less slow', a little faster
tres expressif    - very expressive
un peu retenu     - a little restrained
au Mouvement      - keep in tempo

commencer lentement mais beaucoup animer aussitot -
                  - start slowly but quickly become much livelier

cresc.            - crescendo - becoming louder
assez rapide      - quite fast, rather fast
elargi            - broader, broadened
Moderement lent   - moderately slow
Mouv. du debut    - return to the original tempo

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - slide up
\     - slide down
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
        before the main notes

<x>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret

|-3-| - triplet    - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
|-5-| - quintuplet - 5 notes played in the time of 4 notes
