Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

piano           p    - soft
pianissimo      pp   - very soft
forte           f    - loud
pianississimo   ppp  - extremely soft

CI       barre on the 1st fret
CII      barre on the 2nd fret
CIII     barre on the 3rd fret
CIV      barre on the 4th fret
CV       barre on the 5th fret
CVI      barre on the 6th fret
CVII     barre on the 7th fret
CVIII    barre on the 8th fret
CIX      barre on the 9th fret

poco a poco     - little by little
ten.            - tenuto - sustain the note to its full value, played firmly
dim.            - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
a tempo         - at normal speed
come prima      - in the same manner as the first time
espressivo      - expressively
rit.            - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
sempre          - always, still, continuing
ritard.         - ritardando - a gradual decrease in speed
piu             - more
poco piu mosso  - a little faster
accel.          - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
cresc.          - crescendo - becoming louder
molto           - much, very
moderato        - at a moderate pace

h      - hammer-on
p      - pull-off
/      - slide up
\      - slide down
g      - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
         before the main notes
> > >  - crescendo - becoming louder
< < <  - decrescendo - becoming softer
