Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

mezzo forte   mf   - medium loud
forte         f    - loud
cresc.        crescendo - becoming louder

bpm       - beats per minute (quarter notes)
s         - staccato - detached, cut off, note not held for full value
arp.      - arpeggiate
poco      - a little
rall.     - rallentando - slowing down
a tempo   - at normal speed
t         - tenuto - slightly stressed, played firmly, held for its full length
dim.      - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
F.        - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
attacca   - continue on to the next section without a break

h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
=    - notes held until the end of the following measure
