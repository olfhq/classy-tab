Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The sheet tempo is 120 bpm (beats per minute, quarter notes). The MIDI tempo is
128 bpm, the same speed as the Alfonso Montes reference video.

measure 39 - in the sheet the G and B on the open 3rd and 4th strings are played
             on the 1st beat, but they are not played by Alfonso Montes

left hand fingering (LHF) below tab      right hand fingering (RHF) above tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)            p - thumb
2 - middle finger                        i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger (annular)                m - middle finger
4 - little finger (pinky)                a - ring finger (annular)

===     - note held, length of note, duration
=-=     - let the notes ring on (don't cut them off)
< < <   - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >   - decrescendo - becoming softer
F.      - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
mf      - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
mp      - mezzo-piano - moderately quiet
p       - (below the tab) piano - soft, quietly
pp      - pianissimo - very quiet, very softly
rall.   - rallentando - slowing down (sometimes becoming gradually softer)
tempo   - speed (a tempo - return to the original tempo - 120 bpm)
<7>     - natural (open) harmonic on the 7th fret

c1      - part barre on the 1st fret
C1      - barre on the 1st fret
C2      - barre on the 2nd fret
C2      - part barre on the 2nd fret
