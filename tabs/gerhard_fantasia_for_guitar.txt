non          - not (non arp. - not arpeggiated)

dim.         - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer

marc.        - marcato - with emphasis
subito       - suddenly
sf           - sforzando - strong, sudden accent on a note or chord

meno         - less

/     - slide up

>     - accented, marked, stressed
