performed by Sharon Isbin (1999). Sharon Isbin collected many of the arranged
melodies from renowned Appalachian musicians, including Robin Kessinger, while
she was performing on tour in West Virginia.

1 - index finger              p - thumb
2 - middle finger             i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger               m - middle finger
4 - little finger             a - ring finger (annular)

CII      barre on the 2nd fret
CV       barre on the 5th fret
CVII     barre on the 7th fret
CIX      barre on the 9th fret
[        barre with the index finger

forte    f   - loud
piano    p   - soft

M - (written in the sheet as lower mordents, but played in the reference
    recordings as upper mordents) - ornament - play the main (melody) note,
    followed by the note above (in the scale), and then return to the original
    note - in measures 38 and 78 it is written with a natural sign so should
    be played B-C-B and E-F-E (not B-C#-B and E-F#-E as in the key signature)

arp          - arpeggiate
rit          - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
poco a poco  - little by little
Andante      - at a moderate pace
accel        - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
Tempo I      - return to the original tempo
Maestoso     - in a majestic, dignified manner
subito       - suddenly

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - slide up
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
        before the main notes
>     - accented, marked, stressed
^     - strumming downwards
'     - slight pause (breath mark, luftpause)

<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret
