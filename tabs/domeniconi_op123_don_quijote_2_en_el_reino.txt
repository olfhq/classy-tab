Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

mezzo piano   mp   - medium soft
pianissimo    pp   - very soft
mezzo forte   mf   - medium loud
forte         f    - loud
piano         p    - soft

bpm          - beats per minute (quarter notes) (ca = circa - about, approx)
misterioso   - in a mysterious manner
arp.         - arpeggiate
simile       - in a similar manner
F.           - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
agitando     - agitated, restless
rall.        - rallentando - slowing down
ricordando   - remembering
breve        - (with Fermata) pause for 8 beats
pizz.        - muted form of plucking, with a shorter sustain
               (short, percussive effect rather than sustained)
UM           - ornament - upper mordent - play the main note, followed by the
               note above (in the scale), then return to the original note
nat.         - return to playing normally (after pizz. & m.s. solo)
piu lento    - slower
a tempo      - at normal speed
espr.        - espressivo - expressively
cresc.       - crescendo - becoming louder
stringendo   - gradually faster
cantabile    - in a singing style
ripredendo   - resume previous tempo
m.s. solo    - play with the left hand only
s            - staccato - detached, cut off, note not held for full value
animando     - animated, more lively
attaca       - go straight on to the next movement of the piece

h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
/    - slide up
>    - accented, marked, stressed

<7>    - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 7th fret
<12>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
2[14]  - artificial harmonic, play 2nd fret, point to the 14th fret
3[15]  - artificial harmonic, play 3rd fret, point to the 15th fret
4[16]  - artificial harmonic, play 4th fret, point to the 16th fret
5[17]  - artificial harmonic, play 5th fret, point to the 17th fret
6[18]  - artificial harmonic, play 6th fret, point to the 18th fret

|-3-|  - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
