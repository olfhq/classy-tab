Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
This piece was written by Ponce, but Ponce and Segovia agreed that Segovia
would perform it as a piece supposedly written by Sylvius Leopold Weiss.

differences from the EMT 1673 sheet edition -
measure 3 - in the sheet the 5th note F# on the 4th string (not A)


left hand fingering (LHF)    right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger             p - thumb
2 - middle finger            i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger              m - middle finger
4 - little finger            a - ring finger (annular)

C(n) - barre on the nth fret
c(n) - part barre on the nth fret

h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
/    - slide up
\    - slide down
