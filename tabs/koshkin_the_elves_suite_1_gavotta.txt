Op 26, The Elves Suite - Movement I, Gavotta - Nikita Koshkin (born 1956)
as recorded by Nikita Koshkin
transcribed by Jordan Astridge, August 2004 - elcheimuse@hotmail.com
updated by Jean-Baptiste Rege, March 2025 - see Notes & Legend below
video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzqst6Kvm0 - Marcos Ordonez

This is the first part of Koshkin's "The Elves Suite". It's not supposed to
tell a story, but the Suite expresses what Koshkin imagines of these little
elves. This is a very mysterious and cheeky piece I think.

(checked against the Matanya Ophee sheet edition)
bars 1,5,34 : slide up from A to Bb (3rd string) (not hammer-on)
bar 11      : the trill is played on the 4th string (not the 3rd string)
bar 17      : last interval is F-Bb (not F-B)
bar 19      : the last two chords are Bb-G-C-F (not Bb-G-C#-F)
bar 27      : play with a full barre on the 4th fret
bar 29-31   : slide up the first two notes on the 1st string
bar 43      : slide up the last two notes (from D# to F#)
bar 44      : 1st harmonic played on the 2nd string with the 4th finger
bar 45      : slide up from the G# to the A on the 3rd string
bar 46      : slide up from the C# to the D on the 2nd string
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

=    - note held
>    - accented note
^    - arpeggio
/.\  - fermata
h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
S    - slide
C4   - barre on the 4th fret
<12> - natural harmonic on 12th fret
[x]  - artificial harmonic played one octave higher
