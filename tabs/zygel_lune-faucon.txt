Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The fingering is based on the Eric Franceries reference video and sometimes
differs from the sheet, especially for measures 25-32 and 42-45.

left hand fingering (LHF)          right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger                   p - thumb
2 - middle finger                  i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                    m - middle finger
4 - little finger                  a - ring finger (annular)

CI      barre on the 1st fret
CII     barre on the 2nd fret
CIII    barre on the 3rd fret
CIV     barre on the 4th fret
CV      barre on the 5th fret
CVI     barre on the 6th fret
CVII    barre on the 7th fret

piano           p   - soft
mezzo piano     mp  - medium soft
mezzo forte     mf  - medium loud
forte           f   - loud
pianissimo      pp  - very soft

poco              - a little
sul tasto         - over the fingerboard
rit               - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
a tempo           - at normal speed
ord               - ordinario - discontinue a special effect,
                    return to normal playing
en dehors         - prominently, emphasized
ponticello        - near the bridge
quasi             - similar to
peu a peu         - little by little
se calmant        - calming down, tranquil
dim               - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
Tempo primo       - return to the original tempo
comme un souvenir - like a memory (nostalgic, from a distance)
poco a poco       - little by little
pizz              - muted form of plucking, with a shorter sustain
                    (short, percussive effect rather than sustained)
rall              - rallentando - slowing down
a niente          - fade the music away to little more than a bare whisper
sub               - subito - suddenly
arp               - arpeggiate
F.                - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

h    - hammer-on
p    - (between the notes) pull-off
/    - slide up
\    - slide down
^    - strumming down
>    - accented, marked, stressed
'    - slight pause

<x>   - natural harmonic on the x fret
