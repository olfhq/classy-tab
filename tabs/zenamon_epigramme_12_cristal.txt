Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The tempo of the MIDI file is 88 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).

===    - note held, length of note, duration
> > >  - crescendo - growing louder, gradually becoming louder
> > >  - decrescendo - growing softer, gradually becoming quieter
f      - forte - loud, strong
mf     - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
p      - piano - quietly, soft
pp     - pianissimo - very quietly
rall.  - slowing down, getting slower (sometimes becoming gradually softer)
