Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The MIDI tempo is 100 bpm, slightly slower than the sheet tempo and the
Alfonso Montes reference audio.

measures 9 & 18 - both the sheet and the recording have the last note as C in
                  measure 9 and C# in measure 18 - otherwise measures 8-10
                  are the same as measures 17-19

left hand fingering (LHF) below tab      right hand fingering (RHF) above tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)            p - thumb
2 - middle finger                        i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger (annular)                m - middle finger
4 - little finger (pinky)                a - ring finger (annular)

=====     - note held, length of note, duration
/         - slide the note up (glissando)
arp.      - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
cresc.    - crescendo - growing louder
decresc.  - decrescendo - growing softer
f         - forte - loud, strong
F.        - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
g         - grace note (ornament) - an extra note played before the main note -
            the next note is usually played as a hammer-on, pull-off or slide
h         - hammer-on
mf        - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
p         - (below the tab) piano - soft, quietly
pp        - pianissimo - very soft
rit.      - ritenuto - slowing down
sub.      - subito - suddenly
tempo     - speed (a tempo - return to the original speed)

C1        - barre on the 1st fret
C2        - barre on the 2nd fret [etc]
