Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The order of play is 1-64, 1-15 - measure numbers as in the sheet.

measure 6  - (2nd half) sheet has high frets; tab follows the reference vid
measure 14 - (last 2 notes) sheet has high frets; tab follows the reference vid
measure 22 - if the E (2nd string) is too big a stretch, use the open 1st string
measure 57 - it may be easier to play the last 2 notes (G# A) on the 1st string

left hand fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinky)

appassionato - impassioned, ardently
sempre espressivo - full of expression throughout, play with feeling

===    - note held, duration, length of note
>      - accented, marked, stressed
< < <  - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >  - decrescendo - becoming softer
<12>   - open (natural) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
arp.   - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
D.C.   - Da Capo - return to the start (al Fine - continue until Fine)
dim.   - diminuendo - gradually getting softer
F.     - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their values
Fine   - end (end of the piece)
gliss. - slide the note up or down to the next note, allowing the intervening
         notes to be heard
mf     - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
p      - (below the tab) piano - quiet, soft
p      - (between the notes) pull-off
rit.   - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
tempo  - speed - (a tempo - return to the original tempo)
ten.   - tenuto - held back, note held for at least its full length
c1     - part barre on the 1st fret
C1     - barre on the 1st fret
c2     - part barre on the 2nd fret
C1     - barre on the 2nd fret [etc]
