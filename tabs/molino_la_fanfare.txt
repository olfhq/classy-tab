Notes and Legend
---------------
The tempo of the MIDI is 88 bpm (beats per minute - dotted quarter notes).

left hand fingering (LHF) below tab      right hand fingering (RHF) above tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)            p - thumb
2 - middle finger                        i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger (annular)                m - middle finger
4 - little finger (pinky)                a - ring finger (annular)

===    - note held, length of note, duration
< < <  - (below the tab) crescendo - becoming louder
> > >  - (below the tab) decrescendo - becoming softer
D.C.   - Da Capo - return to the beginning of the piece
f      - forte - loud, strong
Fine   - end, end of the piece (al Fine - continue until Fine)
g      - grace note - ornament - extra note played at the same time as or just
         before the main note then immediately released - in guitar music it is
         usually played on the beat followed by a hammer-on, pull-off or slide
p      - (below the tab) piano - soft, quietly
p      - (between the notes) pull-off
