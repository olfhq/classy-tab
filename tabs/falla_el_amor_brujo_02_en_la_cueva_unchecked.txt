CVII    barre on the 7th fret

pianissimo     pp  - very soft
mezzo-forte    mf  - medium loud
forte          f   - loud
piano          p   - soft

sul tasto  - over the fingerboard
///        - rapid tremolo or repeated rapid alternation on a single note,
             between two notes, or between a note and a chord, executed in
             quick succession, creating a sustained effect
dim        - diminuendo - a gradual decrease in loudness
dolce      - gently, sweetly
marc       - marcato - marked, accented
molto      - much, very
poco       - a little
rit        - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately 
        before the main notes
^     - plucking or strumming downwards
v     - plucking or strumming upwards
>     - accented, marked, stressed
