left hand fingering (LHF) (below tab)    right hand fingering (RHF) (above tab)
1 - index finger                         p - thumb
2 - middle finger                        i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                          m - middle finger
4 - little finger                        a - ring finger (annular)

c1       part barre on the 1st fret
C1       barre on the 1st fret
c2       part barre on the 2nd fret
C2       barre on the 2nd fret (etc)

piano          p   - soft
mezzo-piano    mp  - medium soft
pianissimo     pp  - very soft
mezzo-forte    mf  - medium loud
forte          f   - loud

rubato       - expressive and rhythmic freedom
a tempo      - at normal speed
accel.       - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
rit.         - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
Tempo primo  - return to the original tempo
F.           - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

/      - slide the finger up to the next note
< < <  - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >  - decrescendo - becoming softer
===    - notes held

<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret

|-5-| - quintuplet - 5 notes played in the time of 4 notes
