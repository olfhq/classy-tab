Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
In the reference audio (Rey Guerra) and video (Yoo Sik Ro) measures 64 & 65 are
different to the sheet, and when repeating from the beginning they both jump to
the Coda after measure 10.

left hand fingering (LHF)       right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger                p - thumb
2 - middle finger               i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                 m - middle finger
4 - little finger               a - ring finger (annular)

CI     barre on 1st fret
CII    barre on 2nd fret
CIII   barre on 3rd fret
CIV    barre on 4th fret
CV     barre on 5th fret
CVII   barre on 7th fret
CIX    barre on 9th fret

Lento      - in a slow tempo
Piu mosso  - a little faster
D.C.       - da capo - from the beginning
Coda       - passage that brings a piece to an end,
             a concluding section at the end of a piece

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
>     - accented, marked, stressed
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra note played just before the main note
