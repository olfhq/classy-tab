left hand fingering (LHF)    right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger             p - thumb
2 - middle finger            i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger              m - middle finger
4 - little finger            a - ring finger (annular)

mezzo forte    mf - medium loud
piano          p  - soft
forte          f  - loud
mezzo piano    mp - medium soft
pianissimo     pp - very soft

ostinatamente   - musical phrase repeated throughout the piece
poco            - a little
sul ponticello  - near the bridge
molto           - much, very
dolce           - sweetly, gently
l.v.            - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
subito          - suddenly
m.d. arm        - play the right hand with the right hand
LM              - lower mordent (ornament) - play the main note, followed by
                  the note below (in the scale), then back to the original note
UM              - upper mordent (ornament) - play the main note, followed by
                  the note below (in the scale), then back to the original note
arp.            - arpeggiate
sfz             - sforzato - with particular emphasis

>      - accented, marked, stressed
<12>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
2[14]  - artificial harmonic, play 2nd fret, point to the 14th fret
3[15]  - artificial harmonic, play 3rd fret, point to the 15th fret
