Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
This piece appears in the '35 Preludes', but most performers play a
slightly different version, just called "Lagrima"

order of play: measures 1-8, 1-8, 9-16, 9-16, 1-16, 17-24, 17-24, 1-8

measure 6 - in the sheet the interval on the 1st beat is A-B (not B-B)

left hand fingering (LHF)     right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger              p - thumb
2 - middle finger             i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger               m - middle finger
4 - little finger             a - ring finger (annular)

CII     barre on the 2nd fret
CVII    barre on the 7th fret
CIX     barre on the 9th fret

ritard. - ritardando - a gradual slowing down of tempo
F.      - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
Fine.   - end of the piece

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - slide up
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
        before the main notes

<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
