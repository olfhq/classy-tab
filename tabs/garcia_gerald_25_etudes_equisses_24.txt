Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
This collection of studies is intended for the intermediate guitarist.
The order of play is measures 0-82, 9-47, 83-89
The tempo of the MIDI is 96 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes). Because
much of the rhythm is 3+3+2 (rather than 2+2+2=2) it is better to listen to
the reference videos - the John Holmquist recording is recommeneded.

left hand fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinky)

===      - length of note, duration, note held
< < <    - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >    - decresccendo - becoming softer
$        - segno - sign
+        - Coda symbol (jump to the Coda on repeat)
al Coda  - continue until the Coda symbol (+) then jump to the Coda
arp.     - arpeggiate (ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note)
Coda     - the end section of a piece
D.S.     - Dal Segno - return to the sign ($)
F.       - Fermata - pause - note held for longer than its value
h        - hammer-on
L.V.     - Laissez Vibrer - let vibrate - let it ring, don't dampen the sound
molto    - much, very (rit. molto - suddenly becoming much slower)
morendo  - dying away (gradually becoming softer and softer)
poco     - little (poco a poco - little by little, gradually)
pp       - pianissimo - very soft
rall.    - rallentando - slowing down
rit.     - ritenuto - suddenly slowing down
sempre   - always, throughout the piece
tempo    - speed (a tempo - return to the earlier tempo)

C1  - barre (or part barre) on 1st fret
C2  - barre (or part barre) on 2nd fret [etc]
