Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)     right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger              p - thumb
2 - middle finger             i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger               m - middle finger
4 - little finger             a - ring finger (annular)

CI       barre on the 1st fret
CIII     barre on the 3rd fret
CV       barre on the 5th fret
CVII     barre on the 7th fret
CVIII    barre on the 8th fret
[        barre with the index finger

forte        f   - loud
pianissimo   pp  - very soft

arp   - arpeggiate
pizz  - pizzicato - lightly rest the palm of the right hand
        near the bridge while plucking the strings, for creating
        a muted, percussive sound
M     - upper mordent - ornament - play the main (melody) note,
        followed by the note above (in the scale) and then
        return to the original note
ten   - tenuto - hold, sustain a note for its full length
rit   - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - slide up
\     - slide down
'     - (measure 17) slight pause, breathmark
<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
|-3-| - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
