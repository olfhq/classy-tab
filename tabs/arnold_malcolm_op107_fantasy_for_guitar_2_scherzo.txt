Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)          right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger                   p - thumb
2 - middle finger                  i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                    m - middle finger
4 - little finger                  a - ring finger (annular)

CI      barre on the 1st fret
CIII    barre on the 3rd fret
CIV     barre on the 4th fret
CVI     barre on the 6th fret
CVIII   barre on the 8th fret
CX      barre on the 10th fret
[       part barre

piano                - soft
pianissimo      pp   - very soft
fortissimo      ff   - very loud
mezzo forte     mf   - medium loud
forte           f    - loud
pianississimo   ppp  - very very soft

Allegro   - fast, quickly and bright
sempre    - always, still, continuing
sul pont. - over the fingerboard
arp.      - arpeggiate
pizz.     - muted form of plucking, with a shorter sustain
            (short, percussive effect rather than sustained)
nat.      - natural (discontinue a special effect)
sfz       - sforzando - strong, sudden accent on a note or chord
cresc.    - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
decresc.  - decrescendo - becoming gradually softer
subito    - suddenly
dolce     - gently, sweetly
F.        - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
/    - slide the finger up to the next note
>    - accented, marked, stressed

<12>   - natural harmonic on the 12th fret
<7>    - natural harmonic on the 7th fret
1[13]  - artificial harmonic, play 1st fret, point to the 13th fret

|-8-|  - octuplet - 8 notes played in the time of 6 notes
