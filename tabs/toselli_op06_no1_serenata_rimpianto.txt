Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
"Toselli wrote this piece in 1900, at the age of seventeen based on lyrics
written by Alfredo Silvestri." - Jubing Kristianto

The tab is based on the sheet. Jubing Kristianto plays his own arrangement,
which is in the same key and in parts is similar -
video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m6Tda6Ggwis - Jubing Kristianto

The order of play is 1-18, 10-18, 19-35, 19-33, 36-37, 10-16, 38-41.

measure 41 - in the sheet the bottom note of last chord is F# on 6th string

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

===    - length of note, note held, duration
< < <  - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >  - decrescendo - becoming softer
|~7~|  - septuplet - 7 notes played in the time of 8 notes
Coda   - concluding section, passage that brings a piece to an end
D.S.   - Dal Segno - go back to the sign ($)
g      - grace note (ornament) - an extra note played before the main note -
         the next note is usually played as a hammer-on, pull-off or slide
p      - (below the tab) piano - soft
p      - (between the notes) pull-off
pp     - pianissimo - very soft
rall.  - rallentando - slowing down, broadening out
rit.   - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
tempo  - speed (a tempo - return to the original tempo)
trem.  - (=-=-=-) tremolo - rapid repetition of the note
<9>    - natural (open) harmonic on the 9th fret

c1     - barre on the 1st fret
C1     - part barre on the 1st fret
c2     - part barre on the 2nd fret
C2     - barre on the 2nd fret [etc]

repeat section      1 ___ play the 1st time      2 ___ play on repeat
