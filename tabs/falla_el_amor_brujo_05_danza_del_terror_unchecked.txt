Allegro ritmico  - quickly and with a strong, clear rhythm
marcato          - with emphasis
dim              - diminuendo - a gradual decrease in loudness
pizz             - pizzicato - muted form of plucking, with a shorter sustain
                   (place the right side, the side opposite the thumb, of palm
                   of the right hand on the bridge, slightly resting on the
                   bridge, pluck the strings with the thumb or fingers)
rasg             - rasgueado
piu              - more
cresc            - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
sfz              - sforzando - strong, sudden accent on a note or chord

sempre           - always, continuously
gliss            - glissando
OSSIA            - alternative passage may be played instead
                   of the original passage

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
        before the main notes
^     - strumming or plucking downwards
v     - strumming or plucking upwards
>     - accented, marked, stressed

<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
