measure 26 - the top and bottom notes of the 1st chord are intended to be
             held throughout the measure, but this is very difficult on a
             modern classical guitar - so play the bottom C of the 1st chord
             and then release it before playing the other notes in the chord

left hand fingering (LHF)

3 - ring finger
4 - little finger
