forte           f   - loud

forte piano     fp  - loud soft, hard soft
                      forte followed rapidly by piano
fortissimo      ff  - very loud
piano           p   - soft
pianissimo      pp  - very soft
fortississimo   fff - very very loud


Declamato     - declamatory
bpm           - beats per minute (quarter notes)
rit           - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
F.            - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
cresc         - crescendo - becoming louder
accel         - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
Liberamente   - in a free, unrestrained style
molto         - much, very
Agitato       - agitated, restless, hurried
Stesso tempo  - in the same tempo as before
sempre        - always, still, continuing
rall          - rallentando - slowing down
dolce         - gently, sweetly
tempo giusto  - in strict, exact time
dim           - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
metal         - metalique - near the bridge
ord           - ordinario - return to the usual way of playing
sub           - subito - suddenly
sim           - simile - in a similar manner
sf            - sforzando - strong sudden accent, with particular emphasis
gliss         - glissando
sfff          - subito fortississimo -suddenly very very loud

h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
/    - slide up
\    - slide down
>    - accented, marked, stressed
^    - strum from the lowest note up to the highest note
