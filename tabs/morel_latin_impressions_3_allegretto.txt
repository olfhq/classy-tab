Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CII    barre on the 2nd fret
CIII   barre on the 3rd fret
CV     barre on the 5th fret
CVII   barre on the 7th fret
CIX    barre on the 9th fret

mezzo piano    mp  - medium soft
mezzo forte    mf  - medium loud
forte          f   - loud
piano          p   - soft

Allegretto - moderately fast, at a fairly brisk speed
cresc      - crescendo - becoming louder
arp        - arpeggiate
poco       - a little
rit        - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
a tempo    - at normal speed
F.         - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value -
             (with breve - a long pause)
decresc    - decrescendo - becoming softer, growing quieter

h   - hammer-on
p   - pull-off
g   - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
      before the main notes

|-3-| - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes

<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
