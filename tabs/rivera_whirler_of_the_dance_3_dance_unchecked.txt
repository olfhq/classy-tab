~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

[    barre with the index finger

forte          f   - loud
mezzo-piano    mp  - medium soft
mezzo-forte    mf  - medium loud
piano          p   - soft
fortissimo     ff  - very loud

pizz    - pizzicato - muted form of plucking, with a shorter sustain
          (place the right side, the side opposite the thumb, of palm
           of the right hand on the bridge, slightly resting on the
           bridge, pluck the strings with the thumb or fingers)
ord     - ordinario - return to the normal way of playing
          (discontinue pizzicato)
poco    - a little
cresc   - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
sub     - subito - suddenly
deciso  - in a bold, decided manner
arp     - arpeggiate
sffz    - sforzatissimo - very strongly accented, suddenly very loud

h   - hammer-on
p   - pull-off
/   - slide up
\   - slide down
g   - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
      before the main notes
>   - accented, marked, stressed
