Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The sheet tempo is 84-88 bpm (beats per minute, quarter notes). The MIDI tempo
is 88 bpm. The Alfonso Montes reference recording is faster (~100 bpm).
There are a few very difficult stretches, one of them being in measure 2, but
for the most part it is playable as written. Note the changes in rhythm between
3/4 and 6/8 ( | . | . | . and | . . | . . ). The octave harmonic in measure 85
sounds strange, but this is what Alfonso Montes plays in the reference video.

left hand fingering (LHF) below tab      right hand fingering (RHF) above tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)            p - thumb
2 - middle finger                        i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger (annular)                m - middle finger
4 - little finger (pinky)                a - ring finger (annular)
x - lightly tap nail against wood

=====     - note held, length of note, duration
=-=-=     - let the notes ring on (don't cut them off)
~3~       - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
< < <     - crescendo - becoming louder
arp.      - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
cresc.    - crescendo - growing louder
f         - forte - loud, string
F.        - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
h         - hammer-on
mf        - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
mp        - mezzo-piano - moderately quiet
nat.      - natural - return to playing normally (after sul pont.)
p         - (below the tab) piano - soft, quietly
pp        - pianissimo - very soft
rit.      - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
sim.      - similarly (continue to let the notes ring)
sul pont. - sul ponticello - right hand plays close to the bridge
v         - ripple downwards from the highest note to the lowest note
<7>       - natural harmonic on the 7th fret
<12>      - natural harmonic on the 12th fret

la melodia clara - the clear melody (literal) - emphasise the simple melody

c1        - part barre on the 1st fret
C1        - barre on the 1st fret
c2        - part barre on the 2nd fret
C2        - barre on the 2nd fret [etc]
