Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
original tab by weed@wussu.com - 27 June 2014 - updated 23 February 2025
thanks to Jeff Kollasch for the following corrections -
measures 1,16,17 - D (open 4th string) changed to D#  (4th string 1st fret)

The lute tuning was probably in G, equivalent to using a capo on the 3rd fret.
It is sometimes played with the three bottom strings tuned one octave lower.

The guitar sheet fingering tries to reflect the original lute music. Some of
the stretches and held notes show what the composer intended, but are not
easy to play on a guitar. In these cases the tab has been changed to make it
playable, eg in measure 10, the sheet has the B held for the full measure.

The sheet does not give a tempo. The suggested tempo of 160 bpm is based on the
performance by Marisa Minder, which includes more ornaments than the sheet.
