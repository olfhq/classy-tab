Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
This collection of studies is intended for the intermediate guitarist.
The piece starts in C major and ends in A minor.
The order of play is measures 0-58, 2-55, 59-68.
The tempo of the MIDI is 150 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).

+        - Coda symbol
$        - segno - sign
al Coda  - continue until the Coda symbol (+) then jump to the Coda
c3       - part barre on the 3rd fret
c5       - part barre on the 5th fret
Coda     - the end section of a piece
D.S.     - Dal Segno - return to the sign ($)
F.       - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
g        - grace note (ornament) - acciaccatura - an extra note played as
           quickly as possible before the main note, and immediately released -
           played here as a pull-off
rit.     - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
