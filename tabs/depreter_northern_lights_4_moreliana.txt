Notes and Legend

"...'Northern Lights' presents a rewarding challenge to all advanced players."
- Jan Depreter

order of play: 1-14, 5-13, 15-73, 66-72, 74-98, 5-14, 5-13, 15-37, 101-109

left hand fingering (LHF)          right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger                   p - thumb
2 - middle finger                  i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                    m - middle finger
4 - little finger                  a - ring finger (annular)

CII     barre on the 2nd fret
CIII    barre on the 3rd fret
CIV     barre on the 4th fret
CV      barre on the 5th fret
CVII    barre on the 7th fret
CX      barre on the 10th fret
CXI     barre on the 11th fret
[       part barre (below the tab)

forte           f   - loud
fortissimo      ff  - very loud
piano           p   - soft

Andante       - moderately slow
rall.         - rallentando - becoming slower
rit.          - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
F.            - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
accel.        - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
poco          - a little (poco a poco   - little by little)
arp.          - arpeggiate
espressivo    - expressively
l.v.          - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
sempre        - always, still, continuing
Echo          - like an echo
a tempo       - at normal speed
quasi cadenza - in the manner of a cadenza
cadenza       - improvised or writen-out ornamental passage,
                usually in a 'free' rhythmic style
piu mosso     - faster
cresc.        - crescendo - becoming louder
decresc.      - decrescendo - becoming softer
arrastre      - 'to drag'
sul pont.     - sul ponticello - near the bridge
Allegro       - fast, quickly and bright
ma non tanto  - but not too much so
sub.          - subito - suddenly
D.S.          - Dal segno - from the sign
                (repeat measures 38-100)
piu forte     - louder
CODA          - concluding section, passage that brings the piece to an end
piu lento     - slower
molto         - much, very
Tempo primo   - return to the original tempo
allargando    - gradually slower and more stately

D.S. al @ - return to the % sign (start of measure 5) and continue till @ (the
            CODA symbol) (end of measure 37) then jump to CODA (measure 101)
piu forte e piu mosso - louder and with more motion (livelier) on the repeat

h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
/    - slide up
\    - slide down
>    - accented, marked, stressed
g    - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
       before the main notes

<x>   - natural harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret

|-3-| - triplet    - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
|-5-| - quintuplet - 5 notes played in the time of 4 notes
|-7-| - septuplet  - 7 notes played in the time of 4 notes
