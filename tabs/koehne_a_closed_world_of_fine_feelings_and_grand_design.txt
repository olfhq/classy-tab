Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CII    barre on the 2nd fret
CV     barre on the 5th fret
CVII   barre on the 7th fret
[      barre with the index finger

piano           p    - soft
mezzo forte     mf   - medium loud
pianissimo      pp   - very soft
mezzo piano     mp   - medium soft
pianississimo   ppp  - very very soft

Andante        - moderately slow
teneroso       - tenderly, delicately, softly
rubato         - expressive and rhythmic freedom (temporary disregard strict tempo)
espr           - espressivo - expressively
sim            - simile - in a similar manner
cresc          - crescendo - becoming louder
decresc        - decrescendo - becoming softer
rall           - rallentando - slowing down
dim            - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
F.             - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
poco piu mosso - a little faster
dolcissimo     - very soft
pochiss        - pochissimo - very little, not very much
rit            - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
'              - slight pause, breath mark (luftpause)
a tempo        - at normal speed
tempo I        - return to the original tempo
