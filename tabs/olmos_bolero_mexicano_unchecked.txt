Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CII       barre on the 2nd fret
CIV       barre on the 4th fret
CV        barre on the 5th fret
CVII      barre on the 7th fret
CX        barre on the 10th fret
1/2C(x)   half barre on the x fret

Andante cantabile - moderately slow (at a walking pace), in a singing style
arp               - arpeggiate
F.                - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
poco              - a little
rit               - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
ad lib            - ad libitum - 'at one's pleasure', freely
vib               - vibrar (vibrato) - let the sound of the note continue
                    to ring out and fade naturally, rather than damping it
Deciso            - decisively, with firmness
a tempo           - at normal speed
molto             - much, very
Tempo primo       - return to the original tempo
poco a poco       - little by little

h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
/    - slide up
\    - slide down
g    - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
       before the main notes

<x>  - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret

|-3-| - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes

repeat section       [1. play the 1st time      [2. play on repeat
