Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The order of play is 0-8, 1-7, 9-17, 10-16, 18-26, 19-26, 27-34, 27-33, 35-50.
Measures 36-42 are the same as 1-7, and measures 43-50 the same as 9-16.

measure 3,38 - the first two bass notes are played correctly in the video and
               written correctly in the MIDI (A-A an octave apart), but to my
               ears they sound the same! is this an auditory illusion?

left hand fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger (pinky)

===    - note held, length of note, duration
< < <  - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >  - decrescendo - becoming softer
bpm    - beats per minute (quarter notes)
cresc. - crescendo - becoming louder
f      - forte - loud, strong
mf     - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
p      - (below the tab) piano - quiet, soft
p      - (between the notes) pull-off
rall.  - slowing down (sometimes becoming gradually softer)
tempo  - speed - (a tempo - return to the earlier tempo)

c1   - part barre on the 1st fret
C1   - barre on the 1st fret
c2   - part barre on the 2nd fret
C2   - barre on the 2nd fret [etc]
