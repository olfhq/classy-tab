)  Arpeggios Or Strumming
(

Flam  This means to strum down and up like in flamenco style, usually it's
v ^. Flamenco strumming patterns are usually -
       A) 2 times down(one of them is partial), 1 up , 1 down.
       B) 1 down, 1 up, 1 down
       C) 1 down, 1 up.

There are many ways of strumming: unleashing each finger at a time;
or strumming with all of them at once;
flamenco or traditional-folkloric strumming;
to play on beat according to the intentions of the music;
to follow the rhythm as best as possible;
control strenght, accompany other instruments, etc.
The trick for me is to play it slowly, play along with the original video.
Repeat certain parts many times, for example -
