Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The tempo of the MIDI is 152 bpm (beats per minute - eighth notes)
The order of play is measures 0-8, 0-8, 9-24, 9-24, 25-56, 25-56, 0-24.
The Norbert Neunzling reference video doesn't repeat measures 25-56.

measure 46 - in the sheet the 1st note and the lower note of the interval
             on the 2nd beat are both C (not C#)
measure 54 - in the sheet the lower note of the interval is C (not C#)

1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinkie)

$     - Segno - sign

Fine  - end of the piece (al Fine - continue until Fine, then end)
h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
