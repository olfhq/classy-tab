Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)          right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger                   p - thumb
2 - middle finger                  i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                    m - middle finger
4 - little finger                  a - ring finger (annular)

CI      barre on the 1st fret
CIII    barre on the 3rd fret
CIV     barre on the 4th fret
CV      barre on the 5th fret
CVII    barre on the 7th fret
CIX     barre on the 9th fret

forte          f  - loud
piano          p  - piano
pianissimo     pp - very soft
mezzo forte    mf - medium loud
fortissimo     ff - very loud
mezzo piano    mp - medium soft

apre           - harsh
tres expressif - very expressive
simile         - in a similar manner
molto          - much, very
dim            - diminuendo - diminishing, becoming gradually softer
sffz           - sfozato - strained, the passage should be played with
                 a dynamic emphasis
piu            - more
marque         - with emphasis
sempre         - always, still, continuing
au Mouvement   - at normal speed
gliss          - glissando
passionement   - passionately
gracieux       - graceful
En retenant    - gradually slower, holding back
Un peu retarde - a slight decrease of speed
lointain       - like a distant echo
pizz           - muted form of plucking, with a shorter sustain
                 (short, percussive effect rather than sustained)

h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
/    - slide up
\    - slide down
>    - accented, marked, stressed
g    - grace note (ornament) - extra note(s) played immediately
       before the main notes

<x>    - natural harmonic on the x fret
x[y]   - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret

|-3-| -  triplet   - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
|-6-| -  sextuplet - 6 notes played in the time of 4 notes
|-7-| -  septuplet - 7 notes played in the time of 4 notes
