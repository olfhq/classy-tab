Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CVII    barre on the 7th fret
[       barre with the index finger

piano          p   - soft
fortissimo     ff  - very loud
mezzo-forte    mf  - medium loud

gavotte  - a moderate, graceful French dance popular in the C17th & C18th
rall.    - rallentando - slowing down
a tempo  - at normal speed
cresc.   - crescendo - becoming gradually louder

h   - hammer-on
p   - pull-off
g   - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
      before the main notes
<x> - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
