Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)       right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger                p - thumb
2 - middle finger               i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                 m - middle finger
4 - little finger               a - ring finger (annular)

CII    barre on 2nd fret
CIII   barre on 3rd fret

mezzo piano     mp  - medium soft
pianissimo      pp  - very soft
forte           f   - loud
fortissimo      ff  - very loud
mezzo forte     mf  - medium loud
piano           p   - soft
pianississimo   ppp - very very soft

Tranquillo  - in a quiet manner
l.v.        - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring
a tempo     - at normal speed
S.nat.      - natural, discontinue a special effect
S.tasto     - sul tasto - pluck the strings over the fingerboard
Vivace      - at a brisk, lively tempo
legatissimo - in an exceedingly smooth and connected style, more so than legato
S.ord.      - return to the usual way of performing
cresc.      - crescendo - becoming louder
decresc.    - decrescendo - becoming softer
accell.     - accelerando - accelerating, gradually getting faster
agitato     - in an agitated manner
Tempo I     - return to the original tempo
F.          - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
poco        - a little
sonoro      - sonorous, with a deep, resonant sound
legato      - notes tied together, they should be played in a smooth manner,
              without leaving any space between them
lunga F.    - long pause
              held longer than the music would otherwise indicate
arp.        - arpeggiate
allarg.     - allargando - gradually slower and more stately, broaden the tempo
poco a poco - little by little

/     - slide up
\     - slide down
h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
>     - accented, marked, stressed
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra note played immediately
        before the main note - here played as a slide up
<7>   - natural harmonic on the 7th fret
<12>  - natural harmonic on the 12th fret

|-5-|  - quintuplet - 5 notes played in the time of 4 notes
|-12-| - dodecuplet - 12 notes played in the time of 8 notes
