Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
"Fisher's Hornpipe has been attributed by some sources to an 18th century
composer named John Christian Fisher (1733-1800); another candidate is one
James A Fishar, a musical director and ballet master at London's Covent
Garden during the 1770's. In any event, the earliest cited publication date
(for a tune referred to merely as Hornpipe #1 by J Fishar) is 1778."
- https://bowingdownhome.ca/islandora/object/bdh%3A960

"... it was almost certainly born on the London stage at the hands of a
professional musician and dance master. Though accounts vary, Fisher's likely
was composed by James A Fishar, a professional violinist and ballet master
at Covent Garden, London, who had it published in 1778."
- https://celticjunction.org/cjac/arts-review/issue-18-imbolc-2022/fishers-hornpipe-from-london-to-fort-yukon/
