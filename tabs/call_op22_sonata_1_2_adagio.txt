Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The tempo of the MIDI is 60 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).

measures 5,39,43,51 - Simrock edition: the bottom note of the 2nd chord is G
measures 10,48 - playing the G in the 1st chord as a quarter note is easier -

                    |   .   |   .   |   .
                 e|-------------------------|
                 B|-0===============1=======|
                 G|-0=======0-------0=======|
                 D|-3---0-------3---2-------|
                 A|---------------------3---|
                 E|-------------------------|

measure 18 - both editions have 3rd note C (not the interval A-C as in m.30)
measure 23 - in the Boije edition the 6th note is F (not G)
measure 24 - in the Corbaux edition the 1st chord is G-G-D (not G-F-G-D)
measure 48 - in the equivalent measure 10 the 2nd note is D (not E)

===  - length of note, note held, duration
f    - forte - loud, strong
h    - hammer-on
p    - (between the notes) pull-off
p    - (below the tab) piano - soft, quietly
pf   - play the chord a little louder, with a bit more emphasis
       (pf can mean 'piu forte' or 'poco forte' or 'piano forte')
