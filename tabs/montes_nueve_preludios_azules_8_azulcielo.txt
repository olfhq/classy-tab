Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
measures 5-16 - in the sheet the repeat section is shown as having a different
last measure on the repeat, with the first note being the octave interval A-A
on the 5th and 3rd strings (instead of the A on the 3rd string being held from
the previous measure) - the tab just repeats the whole section, which seems
more natural, so measures 17-91 are equivalent to measures 18-92 in the sheet 

measure 17 - the bottom note of the 3rd chord (G) may sounds strange, but G is
             in the sheet, and Alfonso Montes plays G in the reference video

left hand fingering (LHF) below tab      right hand fingering (RHF) above tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)            p - thumb
2 - middle finger                        i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger (annular)                m - middle finger
4 - little finger (pinky)                a - ring finger (annular)

===      - note held, length of note, duration
>        - accented (in this piece, only slightly accented, not heavily)
^        - strum from lowest note to highest note
V        - strum from highest note to loest note 
\        - (measure 85) glissando - slide the note down
arp.     - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
chas.    - chasquido - like rasquedo (strum) but with the strings muted
cresc.   - crescendo - growing louder
decresc. - decrescendo - growing softer
bpm      - beats per minute (quarter notes)
mf       - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
mp       - mezzo-piano - moderately quiet
p        - piano - quiet, softly

c1       - part barre on the 1st fret
C1       - barre on the 1st fret
c2       - part barre on the 2nd fret
C2       - barre on the 2nd fret [etc]
