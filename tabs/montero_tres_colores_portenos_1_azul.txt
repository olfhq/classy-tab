Left Hand Fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger (pinkie)

====     - note held, length of note, duration
=-=-     - allow the note to continue ringing
>        - accented, marked, stressed
/        - slide the note up
\        - slide the note down
< < <    - (below the tab) crescendo - becoming louder
> > >    - (below the tab) decrescendo - becoming softer
<5>      - natural (open) double octave harmonic on the 5th fret
<12>     - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
arp.     - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
bpm      - beats per minute (quarter notes)
espress. - expressively (molto espress. - with lots of expression)
f        - forte - loud, strong
ff       - fortissimo - very loud
F.       - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
h        - hammer-on
magico   - magically
molto    - much, very
p        - (below the tab) piano - soft, quietly
p        - (between the notes) pull-off
rall.    - rallentando - slowing down (sometimes becoming gradually softer)
t        - tenuto - played firmly, note held for at least its full length
Tam.     - Tambor - play the strings all at once by hitting them (usally near
           the bridge) with the side of the (outstretched) thumb
tempo    - speed (a tempo - return to the earlier speed)
x        - (measure 70) hold the chord on the 1st beat for the tambor

c1       - part barre on the 1st fret
C1       - barre on the 1st fret
c2       - part barre on the 2nd fret
C2       - barre on the 2nd fret [etc]

repeat the section    1____  play the 1st time        2_______  play on repeat
