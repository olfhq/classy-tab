so the MIDI follows the reference video tempo of 50 bpm (quarter notes).

left hand fingering below the tab -
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

===    - note held, length of note, duration
bpm    - beats per minute (quarter notes)
C2     - barre on the 2nd fret
F.     - Fermata - pause, notes held
h      - hammer-on
mf     - mezzoforte - moderately loud
mp     - mezzopiano - moderately soft, medium soft
rit.   - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
