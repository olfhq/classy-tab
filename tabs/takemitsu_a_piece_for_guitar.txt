left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CI     barre on the 1st fret
CII    barre on the 2nd fret
CIII   barre on the 3rd fret

piano         p  - soft
forte         f  - loud
mezzo forte   mf - medium loud
pianissimo    pp - very soft

bpm         - beats per minute (dotted quarter nmotes)
poco        - a little
F.          - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
pont.       - ponticello - near the bridge
ord.        - ordinario - return to playing as normal (end the ponticello)
meno mossso - less rapid
dolce       - gently, sweetly

/  - slide up
\  - slide down
>  - accented, marked, stressed
'  - slight pause (breath mark)
=  - notes held into the following measure

<x>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret
