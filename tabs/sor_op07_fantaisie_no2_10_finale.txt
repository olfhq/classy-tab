Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
This section of the piece is not named "Finale" in the Simrock edition.
Where there are difficult stretches, you can release the chords early or play
the notes on different strings - it was written for a smaller guitar! Also,
Aaron Haas doesn't play all the grace notes, so maybe regard them as optional.

The tempo of the MIDI is 60 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).

===    - note held, length of note, duration
arp.   - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
cresc. - becoming louder
g      - grace note (ornament) - an extra note played immediately before the
         main note, usually played as a hammer-on pull-off or slide
