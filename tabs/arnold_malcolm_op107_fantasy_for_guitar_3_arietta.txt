Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

left hand fingering (LHF)          right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger                   p - thumb
2 - middle finger                  i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger                    m - middle finger
4 - little finger                  a - ring finger (annular)

CV      barre on the 5th fret
CVI     barre on the 6th fret
CVII    barre on the 7th fret
CVIII   barre on the 8th fret

piano           p   - soft
mezzo forte     mf  - medium loud
forte           f   - loud
pianissimo      pp  - very soft

< < <        - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >        - decrescendo - becoming softer
Andante      - moderately slow
con moto     - with movement, in a spirited manner
cantabile    - in a smooth singing style
arp.         - arpeggiate
'            - slight pause (before the next note), breath mark (luftpause)
a tempo      - at normal speed (after the pause)
sul tasto    - over the fingerboard
molto        - much, very
sempre       - always, still, continuing
rallentando  - slowing down
F.           - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

<12>   - natural harmonic on the 12th fret
<19>   - natural harmonic on the 19th fret
2[14]  - artificial harmonic, play 2nd fret, point to the 14th fret
3[15]  - artificial harmonic, play 3rd fret, point to the 15th fret
5[17]  - artificial harmonic, play 5th fret, point to the 17th fret

|-3-| - triplet    - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes
|-5-| - quintuplet - 5 notes played in the time of 4 notes
