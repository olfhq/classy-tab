who live in eternity, who, like me here, hold in your hand, as red as the
heart of God, the sun of each dawn?" - Juan Ramon Jimenez

Allegretto          - at a fairly brisk speed, moderately fast
con entusiasma      - with enthusiasm
arp.                - arpeggiate
subito              - suddenly
cresc.              - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
cedendo             - getting slower
F.                  - Fermata - pause or notes held for longer than their value
Andantino           - slightly faster than Andante
Legato              - notes tied together, they should be played in a smooth manner,
                      without leaving any space between them
espressivo          - expressively
poco piu mosso      - a little faster
rit.                - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
molto               - much, very
sonoro              - sonorous, resonant
a tempo             - at normal speed
eco                 - like an echo
Lento               - at a slow tempo
sfz                 - suddenly loud, strongly accented

h    - hammer-on
p    - (between the notes) pull-off
/    - slide up
>    - accented, marked, stressed
===  - length of notes (notes held)
<19> - natural (open) harmonic on the 19th fret
