Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CIII     barre on the 3rd fret
CV       barre on the 5th fret
CVII     barre on the 7th fret
CVIII    barre on the 8th fret
CX       barre on the 10th fret
[        barre with the index finger

fortissimo    ff - very loud

l.v.        - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring, don't dampen the sound
~M~         - upper mordent (ornament) - play the main note followed by the note
              above (in the scale), and then return to original note
espressivo  - expressively
malinconico - in a melancholy style
arp.        - arpeggiate
F.          - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value

h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
        before the main notes

<12>  - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret
