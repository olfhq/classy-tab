Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The Carlos Barbosa-Lima reference video misses out some of the repeats.

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CII    barre on the 2nd fret
CIII   barre on the 3rd fret
CV     barre on the 7th fret
CVII   barre on the 7th fret
CX     barre on the 10th fret

forte          f   - loud
mezzo forte    mf  - medium loud
mezzo piano    mp  - medium soft
piano          p   - soft

etouffee   - stifled, smothered - (dampen the string gently with the l.h.
             immeadiately after plucking, so the note doesn't sustain)
tastiera   - pluck the strings over the fingerboard
poco rall. - poco rallentando - slowing down a little
arp.       - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from lowest note to highest note
a tempo    - return to the normal speed
cresc.     - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
D.S.al &   - dal segno al & - go back to the sign (%) and play till &
pizz.      - muted form of plucking, with a shorter sustain -
             (place the right side, the side opposite the thumb, of palm
             of the right hand on the bridge, slightly resting on the
             bridge, pluck the strings with the thumb or fingers)
dolce      - gently, sweetly
sul tasto  - over the fingerboard

'     - (measure 4) slight pause (breath mark) before continuing
h     - hammer-on
p     - pull-off
/     - slide up
\     - slide down
g     - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
        before the main notes
>     - accented, marked, stressed

<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret

repeat section     [1. play the 1st time ]    [2. play on repeat    ]
