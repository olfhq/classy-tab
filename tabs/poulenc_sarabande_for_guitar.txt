Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The French guitaristy and composer Ida Presti was born in 1924 (a hundred
years ago). For more info about her see the tonebase video "What Exceptional
Talent Really Means". - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=02AV0sY84Sg

The tempo of the MIDI is 50 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).

measure 21 - in the sheet the 2nd note is G (open 3rd string) but B sounds
             better - Bradford Werner plays G - Stephanie Jones plays B

left hand fingering (LHF) below tab      right hand fingering (RHF) above tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)            p - thumb
2 - middle finger                        i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger (annular)                m - middle finger
4 - little finger (pinky)                a - ring finger (annular)

>      - (above the tab) accented, marked, stressed
^      - ripple upwards from the 5th string to the 4th string with the thumb
=-=    - allow the note to continue ringing
> > >  - decrescendo - becoming softer
C5     - barre on the 5th fret
f      - forte - loud, strong
F.     - Fermata - pause, notes and rests held for longer than their value
mf     - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
p      - (below the tab) piano - soft, quietly
<12>   - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret

Notes

updated 13 June 2024 by weed@wussu.com - measure numbers corrected
In the Ricordi edition of the sheet (LD 557, London / Milan, 1961) measures 3&4,
8&9, 17&18, 23&24 are all reversed, the 1st of the pair being played with a
barre on the 5th fret, and the 2nd of the pair being played on the lower frets.
