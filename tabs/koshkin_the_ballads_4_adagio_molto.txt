Notes
~~~~~

BAR 13 : for a great transition from D# to A, lift the chord to play the bass
again, slowly rotating your wrist while you sustain D# as much as you can before
playing the A. I'd even play it with the side of the finger to perform a fast
"quasi-slide" if you still hear the transistion.

BAR 21 : lift only the 1st phalanx of your index to keep the bass part ringing.
