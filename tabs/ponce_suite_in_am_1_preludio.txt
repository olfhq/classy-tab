Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
This piece was written by Ponce, but Ponce and Segovia agreed that Segovia
would perform it as a piece supposedly written by Sylvius Leopold Weiss.
The time signature in the sheet is 3/4, but the notes are written as if
the time signature was 9/8.

measure 31 - in the sheet the 3rd & 9th notes are C (not C#)

left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

C2  - barre on the 2nd fret
C3  - barre on the 3rd fret
C5  - barre on the 5th fret
C7  - barre on the 7th fret

h   - hammer-on
p   - pull-off
/   - slide up
