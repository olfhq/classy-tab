Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

CIII   barre on the 3rd fret
CIV    barre on the 4th fret
CV     barre on the 5th fret
CVI    barre on the 6th fret
CVII   barre on the 7th fret
CIX    barre on the 9th fret

pianississimo   ppp  - very very soft
mezzo forte     mf   - medium loud
fortissimo      ff   - very loud
mezzo piano     mp   - medium soft
pianisssimo     pp   - very soft

Larghetto - moderately slow and broad
arp.      - arpeggiate - ripple upwards from lowest note to highest note
rit.      - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
a tempo   - at normal speed
accel.    - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
F.        - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
cresc.    - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
rubato    - expressive and rhythmic freedom without losing the overall tempo

>  - accented, marked, stressed
h  - hammer-on

<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret
