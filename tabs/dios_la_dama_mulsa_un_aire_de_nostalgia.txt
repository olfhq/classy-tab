Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The left hand fingering in the tab follows the Javier Riba video, which
in places is different from the sheet - (eg in the sheet measures 17 and
18 are played on the low frets).

left hand fingering (LHF)     right hand fingering (RHF)
1 - index finger              p - thumb
2 - middle finger             i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger               m - middle finger
4 - little finger             a - ring finger (annular)

CIII    barre on the 3rd fret
CIV     barre on the 4th fret
CVII    barre on the 7th fret
[       barre with the index finger

mezzo-forte    mf  - medium loud
pianissimo     pp  - very soft
piano          p   - soft
fortissimo     ff  - very loud
forte          f   - loud
mezzo-piano    mp  - medium soft

bpm      - beats per minute - quarter notes
F.       - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
poco     - a little
rit.     - ritenuto - slowing down, suddenly slower
a tempo  - return to playing at the normal speed
arp.     - arpeggiate
lejano   - far, distant
sempre   - always (sempre cresc. - continuing to get louder)
cresc.   - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
Ad lib   - ad libitum - 'at liberty' - the tempo is left to the performer
Tempo I  - (tempo - speed) return to the original tempo

h  - hammer-on
p  - pull-off
/  - slide up
g  - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately
     before the main notes

<12>  - natural (open) octave harmonic on the 12th fret

|-3-| - triplet - 3 notes played in the time of 2 notes

 repeat section
