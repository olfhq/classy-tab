The tempo of the MIDI file is 66 bpm (beats per minute - dotted quarter notes).

measure 17 - in the sheet the G at the bottom of the 1st interval is held to
             the end of the measure

measures 25,26,29,30 - not holding on to the G at the bottom of the 1st chord
                       makes it much easier to play the next 2 notes
