Notes & Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The tempo of the MIDI is 60 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).
The order of play is measures 0-55, 1-29. The key signature in the
sheet doesn't change but the key changes to A major at measure 30.

===    - note held, length of note, duration
$      - Segno - sign
D.S.   - Dal Segno - return to the sign ($ - measure 1)
dolce  - sweetly
f      - forte - loud, strong
F.     - Fermata - pause - rests are held for longer than their value
Fine   - end of the piece (al Fine - continue until Fine)
p      - (below the tab) piano - quiet, soft
