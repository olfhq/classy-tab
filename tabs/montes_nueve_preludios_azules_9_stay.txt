Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

measures 36-38,40,41 - the rhythm changes to 3+3+2 eight beats per measure

left hand fingering (LHF) below tab      right hand fingering (RHF) above tab
1 - index finger (forefinger)            p - thumb
2 - middle finger                        i - index finger (forefinger)
3 - ring finger (annular)                m - middle finger
4 - little finger (pinky)                a - ring finger (annular)

===     - note held, length of note
< < <   - crescendo - becoming louder
> > >   - decrescendo - becoming softer
|-3-|   - triplet (3 notes in the time of 2 notes)
/       - glissando - slide the note up
\       - glissando - slide the note down
bpm     - beats per minute (quarter notes)
c1      - part barre on the 1st fret
C1      - barre on the 1st fret
C3      - barre on the 3rd fret
C8      - barre on the 8th fret
f       - forte - loud, strong
F.      - Fermata - pause, note held for longer than its value
h       - hammer on
l.v.    - laissez vibrer (let vibrate) - let it ring, don't dampen the sound
mf      - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
mp      - mezzo-piano - moderately quiet
p       - piano - soft, quietly
pp      - pianissimo - very soft
sempre  - always (contnue playing theis way throughout the piece)
tam.    - tambora - play the strings all at once by hitting them sharply
          near the bridge with the side of the thumb
vibrato - enriching the sound of a note by vibrating the finger laterally
          along the direction of the string
