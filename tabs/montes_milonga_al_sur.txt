C(n)    - barre on the nth fret
c(n)    - part barre on the nth fret

arp.    - arpeggiate, ripple upwards from the lowest note to the highest note
+       - (end of measure 17) Coda symbol
F.      - Fermata - pause, notes held for longer than their value
D.C.    - Da Capo - return to the beginning of the piece
al Coda - continue playing until the Coda symbol then jump to the Coda
Coda    - passage that brings a piece to an end, concluding passage

h  - hammer-on
p  - pull-off
'  - (end of measure 28) slight pause
/  - slide up
\  - slide down
^  - strumming upwards (lowest note to highest note)
v  - strumming downwards (highest note to lowest note)
