Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The left hand fingering is from the sheet, and is not always the same as in
the David Tutmark video reference.
The tempo of the MIDI is 132 bpm (beats per minute - quarter notes).
The order of play is measures 1-16, 1-16, 17-32, 17-32.

left hand fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinky)

===  - length of note, note held, duration
/    - slide up to the next note
C2   - barre on the 2nd fret
c5   - part barre on the 5th fret
g    - (measure 6) - grace note (ornament) - an extra note played before the
       main note - here played as a hammer-on
