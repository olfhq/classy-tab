Notes & Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

fingering :

h  - hammer-on
p  - pull-off
g  - grace note
>  - accented note
^  - arpeggio
S  - slide
=  - note sustained
~  - vibrato
VI - full barre on 6th fret
vi - small barre on 6th fret
h  - natural harmonic

expressions :

cresc     - louder
dim       - quieter
graz      - lit. "graciously", with elegance
sul pont  - play really close to the bridge
sul tasto - play over the fretboard
dolce     - mellowy, softly
marcato   - loud, more forcefully

dynamics :

/.\ - fermata
.   - staccato
_   - tenuto
rit - slowing down
acc - accelerating
sub - suddenly
pap - poco a poco - little by little

fingerings :

h  - hammer-on                              in notes :
p  - pull-off
g  - grace note                             # - rest / note extinction
>  - accented note
^  - arpeggio
S  - slide
=  - note sustained
~  - vibrato
VI - full barre on 6th fret
vi - small barre on 6th fret
h  - natural harmonic

nuances :

cresc     - louder
dim       - quieter
graz      - lit. "graciously", with elegance
sul pont  - play really close to the bridge
sul tasto - play over the fretboard
dolce     - mellowy, softly
marcato   - loud, more forcefully

dynamics :

/.\ - fermata
.   - staccato
_   - tenuto
rit - slowing down
acc - accelerating
sub - suddenly
pap - bit by bit

PERFORMANCE NOTES

-   in order to keep this crowded tab as legible as I can, I will show sustained notes in only two instances :
    throughout the bassline as it's important for the piece
    for the melody and accompaniment, only when you should hold a note while playing something else


-   rythm  :  note spacing is quite accurate
              x-        16th note                 keep in mind this is a bit broken when dealing with notes on frets 10 and up and grace notes
              x---      8th note                  I tried to keep the correct spacing on the melody as much as possible
              x-----    dotted 8th
              x-------  quarter note              also, fingering notation doesn't interfere with the spacing. So |-8S10--| is two 16th with a slide
              x---------dotted quart. (whole bar)

              -|- is the whole bar line for legibility, so -|-5h6-5-7--|- would be all 16th notes


-   there are quite a lot of portamenti that go above the body of the guitar. Ideally, let go of the neck with your thumb at the end of the slide (or rest it on the
    splint right next to the neck if you have gigantic hands)


-   natural harmonics on the 4th fret are played a few millimeters above the fret. They're a bit harder to play as your finger placement must be quite precise.


-   the funky stuff starting at bar 170 is a long semi-random glissando on the B string. In the score it's notated as a long squiggle ; you're meant to mimic the line curvature with your
    left hand. i.e. In practice you're kind of encouraged to do as you see fit.
    right-hand wise, You could strike the B string every bar as you play the bass or if you want to follow the score, strike every 16th note.
    I higly suggest you check Frank Koonce's recording on the "well tempered koshkin" album, as he follows the score rather strictly, but he accentuates the glissando
    while playing the bass. This works quite well imo.
    Again, I think you can absolutely take the notation / accents very liberally in this section.

fingerings :

h - hammer-on                              in notes :
p - pull-off
g - grace note                             # - rest / note extinction
> - accented note
^ - arpeggio
S - slide
= - note sustained
~ - vibrato
VI - full barre on 6th fret
vi - small barre on 6th fret
h - natural harmonic

nuances :

cresc - louder
dim - quieter
graz - lit. "graciously", with elegance
sul pont - play really close to the bridge
sul tasto - play over the fretboard
dolce - mellowy, softly
marcato - loud, more forcefully

dynamics :

/.\ - fermata
. - staccato
_ - tenuto
rit - slowing down
acc - accelerating
sub - suddenly
pap - bit by bit

PERFORMANCE NOTES

-   in order to keep this crowded tab as legible as I can, I will show sustained notes in only two instances :
    throughout the bassline as it's important for the piece
    for the melody and accompaniment, only when you should hold a note while playing something else


-   rythm  :  note spacing is quite accurate
              x-        16th note                 keep in mind this is a bit broken when dealing with notes on frets 10 and up and grace notes
              x---      8th note                  I tried to keep the correct spacing on the melody as much as possible
              x-----    dotted 8th
              x-------  quarter note              also, fingering notation doesn't interfere with the spacing. So |-8S10--| is two 16th with a slide
              x---------dotted quart. (whole bar)

              -|- is the whole bar line for legibility, so -|-5h6-5-7--|- would be all 16th notes


-   there are quite a lot of portamenti that go above the body of the guitar. Ideally, let go of the neck with your thumb at the end of the slide (or rest it on the
    splint right next to the neck if you have gigantic hands)


-   the funky stuff starting at bar 170 is a long semi-random glissando on the B string. In the score it's notated as a long squiggle ; you're meant to mimic the line curvature with your
    left hand. i.e. In practice you're kind of encouraged to do as you see fit.
    right-hand wise, You could strike the B string every bar as you play the bass or if you want to follow the score, strike every 16th note.
    I highly suggest you check Frank Koonce's recording on the "well tempered koshkin" album, as he follows the score rather strictly, but he accentuates the glissando
    while playing the bass. This works quite well imo.
    Again, I think you can absolutely take the notation / accents very liberally in this section.
