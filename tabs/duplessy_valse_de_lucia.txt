h    - hammer-on                           LEFT HAND     |  RIGHT HAND
p    - pull-off                                          |
>    - accented note                       1 = index     |  p = thumb
r    - rasgueado                           2 = middle finger |  i = index
=    - note sustained                      3 = ring finger   |  m = ring finger
VI   - full barre on 6th fret              4 = pinky     |  a = pinky
vi   - small barre on 6th fret
