Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The vids make those stretches look easy, but they're tough!
The order of play is measures 1-14, 1-14, 15-30, 15-30, 31-49.

measure 14 - LHF - last note, use 4th finger on repeat
measure 30 - LHF - last note, use 3rd finger on repeat

Left Hand Fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger (pinkie)

===      - length of note, note held, duration
> > >    - becoming softer
bpm      - beats per minute (quarter notes)
C1       - barre on the 1st fret
cresc.   - crescendo - becoming gradually louder
F.       - Fermata - pause, note held for longer than its value
h        - hammer-on
mp       - mezzo-piano - moderately soft
p        - pull-off
poco     - little, a little (poco rit. - a little slower)
rit.     - ritenuto - slower, suddenly slower
ritard.  - ritardando - slowing down gradually
sost.    - sostenuto - sustained, let the note continue to sound
tempo    - speed (a tempo - return to the earlier tempo)
